## Findings vs Existing Claims
### F2.1: 2025 comprehensive review consolidates MMP-9 as multi-mechanism pronociceptive driver
- **Evidence:** Systematic review of MMP-9 across peripheral neuropathic pain models: rapid upregulation in injured DRG neurons, IL-1β cleavage, microglial activation via p38, BSCB/BBB opening, TRPV1 sensitization, CX3CL1 cleavage, NLRP3 inflammasome activation (mechanical allodynia), EZH2/TIMP1-MMP-9 imbalance in CCI, and clinical plasma MMP-9 correlations with postoperative pain severity [Frontiers Mol Neurosci 2025, doi:10.3389/fnmol.2025.1647316].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0070 (MMP-9→PV damage via PNN degradation), EC-0071 (BBB tight junctions), EC-0074 (ROCK), EC-0151 (NgR1 upstream) → SUPPORTS (core claims reconfirmed and broadened to Nav1.7/NMDAR/NLRP3 effector arms).

### F2.2: ELF1 identified as injury-induced transcriptional activator of Mmp9 in DRG
- **Evidence:** Nerve trauma elevates E74-like factor 1 (ELF1) in L3/4 DRG; AAV-shRNA knockdown of Elf1 blocked CCI-induced MMP-9 upregulation and nociceptive hypersensitivity in both sexes; ELF1 binds the Mmp9 promoter directly; proposed antisense-oligonucleotide target upstream of MMP-9 [E74-like factor 1 study, PMC9633582, ~J Neurosci 2022/2023].
- **Relation to KB:** Upstream of EC-0072/EC-0073/EC-0074 MMP-9 hub → NEW_MECHANISM (adds a druggable transcriptional initiator above the KB's existing MMP-9 node).

### F2.3: Microglial GAG stripping of lamina I spinoparabrachial PNNs is sufficient for pain (landmark)
- **Evidence:** After SNI, microglia remove glycosaminoglycans from PNNs enwrapping lamina I spinoparabrachial projection neurons (aggrecan core preserved); microglia depletion prevents GAG loss and hypersensitivity; genetic PNN disruption or local chondroitinase ABC at lamina I reproduces spontaneous pain + thermal hypersensitivity without microglial activation; no male/female difference observed [Tansley et al., Science 2022;377(6601):80-86, doi:10.1126/science.abl6773; commentary doi:10.1038/s41392-022-01162-0]. Companion ALS work shows reactive microglia expressing MMP-9 phagocytose aggrecan+ nets around motor neurons [Cheung et al., cited in doi:10.1007/s11064-025-04506-8].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0070 → SUPPORTS with mechanistic refinement: the dominant early degradative step is phagocytic GAG removal by microglia rather than direct aggrecan-core proteolysis by MMP-9; also tempers the KB's sex-difference caution (PNN_ECM.md) — none detected for this mechanism.

### F2.4: Cortical/thalamic PNN build-up drives sensitization — region-specific sign inversion
- **Evidence:** In CCI mice, WFA+ PNN density increased contralaterally in somatosensory cortex, mPFC, insula and reticular thalamic nucleus (with brevican/neurocan upregulation); local chABC in SSC or RTN raised mechanical/thermal thresholds (analgesic), whereas MMP-9 siRNA in SSC/RTN of sham mice lowered pain thresholds — i.e., basal cortical MMP-9 tone is anti-nociceptive there [Mascio et al., Pain 2025, PMC12004981]; same group proposes an mGlu5–PNN–PV-interneuron axis maintaining overactive cortical PV cells that suppress descending analgesic pathways [doi:10.1186/s44158-025-00228-z, 2025].
- **Relation to KB:** CONTRADICTS the generalized reading of EC-0070 / PNN_ECM.md cortical section ("cortical PNN degradation → disinhibition → pain"): in SSC/mPFC/RTN the direction reverses (PNN accumulation = pronociceptive; degradation = analgesic). Spinal lamina I sign (F2.3) remains intact. The KB graph needs region-conditioned edge signs.

### F2.5: Pharmacological rescue of PNN integrity via microglial-phagocytosis checkpoints
- **Evidence:** Three 2025-2026 studies converge on blocking microglial PNN engulfment as analgesic strategy: (i) S-ketamine reduced CCI pain, neuroinflammation and microglia-mediated PNN phagocytosis (IBA1/CD68 engulfing WFA+ material, 3D reconstruction) [PMID 41023268, 2025]; (ii) 11-keto-β-boswellic acid + Z-guggulsterone suppressed HMGB1/TLR4-MyD88-p65 signaling, repolarized microglia and reversed PNN degradation in CCI mice [PMID 41187808, J Adv Res 2025]; (iii) Tuina therapy suppressed CX3CL1/CX3CR1-PI3K/AKT, reduced TREM2/LAMP1 phagocytic markers, restored WFA staining [doi:10.3389/fnmol.2026.1860375]. A 2025 review maps additional regulators: IL-33/IL1RL1-driven ECM engulfment, CSF1R tone, TGFβ-after-BBB-disruption PNN loss [doi:10.1007/s11064-025-04506-8].
- **Relation to KB:** EXTENDS PNN_ECM.md "Microglial Phagocytosis of PNNs" mechanism into actionable targets → NOVEL_TARGET (TREM2/LAMP1 phagolysosomal axis; HMGB1/TLR4; CX3CR1; IL-33/IL1RL1; CSF1R).

### F2.6: HAPLN1/Crtl1 necessity for spinal PNN integrity and pain thresholds
- **Evidence:** Crtl1-knockout mice show aberrant, thin spinal PNNs with baseline hyperreflexia and hyperalgesia phenocopying SCI outcomes; link protein 1 also mediates activity-dependent adult PNN remodeling after exercise/injury [Sánchez-Ventura et al., Int J Mol Sci 2024;25:4267, doi:10.3390/ijms25084267]. New live-imaging tools (HaloTag-HAPLN1 probe; HAPLN1-Venus knock-in) enable longitudinal ECM tracking, and viral HAPLN1 delivery is reported necessary/sufficient to accelerate PNN maturation [PMC11502233, 2024; PMC10705503, 2023]; Crtl1 loss alone renders fear memories erasable [Poli et al., Mol Neurobiol 2023, doi:10.1007/s12035-023-03314-x].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0098 (PNNs critical for normal function) and PNN_ECM.md "Restoring PNN Synthesis via aggrecan/HAPLN1 vectors" → SUPPORTS (necessity now demonstrated in vivo at spinal level; repair-tooling maturing).

### F2.7: Gelatinase inhibitors reach translational maturity — SB-3CT lineage superseded by AZD1236
- **Evidence:** SB-3CT (mechanism-based MMP-2/9 gelatinase inhibitor) reduced MMP-9 activity, BSCB disruption and apoptosis after SCI but is rapidly metabolized to an inactive species limiting use; next-generation oral/intrathecal AZD1236 (MMP-9+MMP-12) given within 24 h-3 d post-SCI reduced edema ~98%, pro-nociceptive markers ~80%, alleviated neuropathic pain and restored sensory/locomotor function; clinical translation attempts underway; historical musculoskeletal-syndrome fears attributed to non-selective first-generation MMPi are being revisited ["Reviving the use of inhibitors of MMPs in SCI", PMC10233756, 2023; Yu 2008 SB-3CT and Ahmed 2022 AZD1236 data therein]. 2025 review lists BB-1101/SB-3CT preclinical analgesia and flags selectivity/bioavailability as remaining barriers [doi:10.3389/fnmol.2025.1647316].
- **Relation to KB:** PNN_ECM.md "MMP Inhibition Restores PNNs and Relieves Pain" (SB-3CT/TIMP-1) and "Clinical Implications" caution → SUPPORTS; extends target definition to dual MMP-9/12 blockade and time-window framing → NOVEL_TARGET (AZD1236-class, windowed dosing).

### F2.8: Aggrecanase neopepitopes (NITEGE/ARGS) are validated biomarkers of aggrecanolysis linked to pain — but not yet in neuropathic pain
- **Evidence:** ADAMTS-generated NITEGE373 and MMP-generated VDIPEN341 neoepitopes mark early cartilage catabolism; notably, naive mouse peripheral nerves and brain showed intense anti-NITEGE373 labeling, indicating CNS aggrecanase activity [PMID 9536289, 1998]. GMP-grade huARGS chemiluminescence assay quantifies ADAMTS-4/5-cleaved ARGS-G2 in serum and tracks response to the anti-ADAMTS-5 nanobody M6495 [PMC9718149, 2022]; ARGS biological variation characterized in OA/ACL cohorts [PMC9718341, 2022]. Mechanistically, aggrecan destruction fragments activate TLR-2 → induce IL-6 → contribute to pain, providing a temporal link between aggrecanolysis and pain symptoms [Curr Opin Rheumatol 2022 OA biomarker update, journals.lww.com/co-rheumatology/fulltext/2022/01000].
- **Relation to KB:** PNN_ECM.md "Biomarker Potential: aggrecan fragments in CSF analogous to cartilage markers" → EXTENDS (assays and pain-fragment signaling now exist clinically for cartilage; CNS/PNN adaptation untested). No NITEGE/ARGS measurements in neuropathic-pain CSF were found.

### F2.9: Enzymatic PNN/GAG manipulation confirms causality — and reveals Ca²⁺ overload phenotype
- **Evidence:** Intrathecal chABC in naïve-context CCD rats reproduced the model's full hyperalgesic trajectory: selective WFA-GAG loss with unchanged aggrecan core protein, elevated neuronal Ca²⁺, c-FOS induction and mitochondrial ultrastructural damage in dorsal horn [doi:10.3389/fnmol.2026.1860375, 2026]. Conversely, in SCI repair settings chABC promotes sprouting/recovery without inducing mechanical allodynia or thermal hyperalgesia [Cafferty et al., J Neurosci 2008;28:11998, doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3877-08.2008; J Neurosci 2006;26:10856; NPC+ChABC combo "did not exacerbate posttraumatic neuropathic pain", J Neurosci 2010;30:1657, doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3111-09.2010; primate Chase trial safe, Nat Neurosci 2019, doi:10.1038/s41593-019-0424-1]. At the peripheral interface, hyaluronidase and low-molecular-weight hyaluronan induce mechanical hyperalgesia via CD44 signaling [PMC5365238, 2017].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0093 (Ca²⁺ overload ↔ PNN loss) → SUPPORTS (direct experimental demonstration of PNN-degradation→Ca²⁺-overload direction); PNN_ECM.md hyaluronidase/chABC causal experiments → SUPPORTS, with important context-dependence (see Contradictions).

### F2.10: Phase-specific MMP targeting framework holds after 17 years
- **Evidence:** Landmark re-read through 2023-2025 reviews: MMP-9 rapid/transient DRG-neuron upregulation gates early-phase allodynia (TIMP-1, siRNA, KO, inhibitor-I all effective early; exogenous i.t. MMP-9 sufficient), while MMP-2 (satellite cells/astrocytes) maintains late phase (TIMP-2 effective late) via IL-1β cleavage; MMP-3 can activate proMMP-9 upstream in spinal inflammatory pain [Kawasaki et al., Nat Med 2008;14:331-336, doi:10.1038/nm1723, reconfirmed in doi:10.3390/biom13020268 (2023), doi:10.3389/fnmol.2025.1647316 (2025), doi:10.3389/fmolb.2024.1382555 (2024)].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0070..EC-0074 temporal structure → EXTENDS (KB dossier lacks explicit early/late phase assignment; graph should encode phase windows on MMP edges).

## Contradictions & Contested Areas
1. **Region-specific sign inversion (strongest conflict):** Spinal lamina I PNN degradation promotes pain [Tansley 2022], but forebrain PNN build-up accompanies neuropathic pain and its enzymatic removal is analgesic in SSC/RTN [Mascio, Pain 2025] — opposite to the KB's uniform "PNN loss = disinhibition = pain" framing, including the PNN_ECM.md ACC/mPFC section. Graph must condition PNN-degradation→pain edges on region/cell class.
2. **Basal MMP-9 tone in cortex:** MMP-9 knockdown lowered pain thresholds in sham mice (SSC/RTN) [Mascio 2025], contradicting an unqualified "MMP-9 = pronociceptive" edge; consistent with MMP-9's physiological plasticity roles.
3. **CX3CR1 dependence of PNN phagocytosis:** Tuina study treats CX3CL1/CX3CR1-PI3K/AKT as the key PNN-degradation axis, whereas the 2025 review states this axis "does not appear essential" for SNI-induced spinal PNN degradation (and not required in ALS models), implicating IL-33/IL1RL1 instead — unresolved.
4. **Sex differences:** KB caution section suggests possibly greater MMP-9-mediated PNN reliance in males; Tansley 2022 found no male/female differences in microglial PNN degradation driving pain.
5. **ChABC bidirectionality:** chABC induces pain-like behavior when applied to intact/near-intact nociceptive circuitry (lamina I, cortex context differs) yet is analgesic/pro-regenerative and does not provoke allodynia after SCI — timing-and-lesion dependence must be encoded, echoing the KB's therapeutic-window caveat.
6. **Methodological caveat:** WFA-loss with preserved aggrecan core (Tansley 2022; Tuina 2026) means lectin-only readouts overstate structural degradation; KB claims citing WFA alone should be annotated.

## Landmark Classics Reconfirmed
- **Kawasaki et al. 2008, Nat Med** (early MMP-9 / late MMP-2 phases; TIMP-1/2 anti-allodynic; exogenous MMP-9 sufficiency) — fully reconfirmed and still the backbone of 2023-2025 reviews. Note: the sweep brief's "Kawaji MMP-9 2005" could not be located in any search; closest match is this Kawasaki 2008 paper — flagging probable citation conflation in the brief.
- **Tansley et al. 2022, Science** (microglia degrade lamina I PNNs of spinoparabrachial projection neurons → pain) — confirmed at doi:10.1126/science.abl6773; now extended by 2025-2026 phagocytosis-targeting therapeutics.
- **Bradbury-lineage chABC work** (Bradbury 2002 Nature; Cafferty/Massey 2006-2008; primate Chase 2019) — reconfirmed as the CSPG-repair canon, including the negative finding that chABC sprouting does not create allodynia after dorsal column injury.
- **Dityatev-lineage PNN formation work** — Dityatev 2007 Dev Neurobiol (activity/Ca²⁺-dependent PNN assembly; chABC increases interneuron excitability) reconfirmed; however, a specific "Dityatev 2010 chABC allodynia" primary paper did not surface (see Limitations).
- **NITEGE/VDIPEN neoepitope system (van Meurs-era, 1998)** — revalidated as specific protease-activity readouts; CNS anti-NITEGE labeling in normal tissue noted since the original work.

## Sweep Limitations
- Two landmark anchors from the brief could not be verified: **no "Kawaji 2005" MMP-9 paper** surfaced (likely conflation with Kawasaki et al. 2008 Nat Med) and **no "Dityatev 2010 chABC allodynia" paper** was retrieved (nearest: Dityatev 2007 culture work; ECM-remodeling review 2010). KB source notes referencing these should be audited.
- No 2021-2026 primary study directly measuring **CSF/blood NITEGE or ARGS in neuropathic pain patients** was found; the biomarker claim rests on cartilage-field assays plus incidental CNS NITEGE staining — extrapolation risk flagged.
- Several 2025 records were visible only as PubMed abstracts or search snippets (S-ketamine PMID 41023268; KBA/Z-GS PMID 41187808; Mascio Pain 2025 partially paywalled); journal names/DOIs incomplete where the search results did not show them (URL fallback used).
- PMC mirrors occasionally returned bot-check pages (e.g., "Perineuronal nets in the brain: New vistas in pain mechanisms", PMC12003085), so that review was not deeply read.
- The KB's specific claim that chABC injection into the **naïve** spinal dorsal horn induces allodynia/hyperalgesia (PNN_ECM.md) was only indirectly supported (Tansley lamina I chABC; intrathecal chABC in CCD rats); a dedicated naïve-animal dorsal-horn chABC behavioral paper was not independently retrieved in this sweep.
- Search coverage skewed English-language, rodent/preclinical literature; clinical MMP/PNN intervention trials (if any beyond AZD1236 translation attempts) may be underrepresented.
