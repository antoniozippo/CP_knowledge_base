## Findings vs Existing Claims

### F1.1: Clinical translation has moved from tool compounds to named development candidates — but no CLP257 trial exists
- **Evidence:** As of the Dec 2024 Frontiers review, no FDA-approved KCC2 potentiator exists and no clinical trial of CLP257 itself was found; instead Axonis' oral AXN-027 entered first-in-human trials (Q4 2024, planned PoC in chronic epilepsy and pain) and Ovid's IV OV350 was slated for first testing H1 2025 [Pressey/Hegarty-lab authors, Front. Mol. Neurosci. 2024, doi:10.3389/fnmol.2024.1503070].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0039..QC-0046 (KCC2_enhancers_new), EC-0099 → EXTENDS (pipeline status beyond preclinical CLP290/ARN23746 claims).

### F1.2: Mechanism-of-action debate on CLP257; repurposed drugs enhance KCC2 by membrane clustering
- **Evidence:** A follow-up study is titled "The small molecule CLP257 does not modify activity of the K+–Cl− cotransporter…" (i.e., indirect mechanism), while a 2026 PNAS paper identifies prochlorperazine and CLP-257 as enhancing KCC2 function by promoting its membrane clustering [PMC7371006, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7371006/; PNAS 2026, doi:10.1073/pnas.2522722123].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0040/QC-0042 (potency/selectivity statements) → NOVEL_TARGET (prochlorperazine repurposing edge) with a MOA caveat to attach to CLP257 claims.

### F1.3: Human dorsal horn shows decreased KCC2 in chronic pain and long-term opioid use
- **Evidence:** Human post-mortem/surgical SDH tissue shows decreased KCC2 expression associated with chronic pain and long-term opioid use, supporting that reduced dorsal-horn inhibitory efficacy promotes chronic pain in humans [Davis et al., bioRxiv 2025, doi:10.1101/2025.01.19.633733; journal version indexed as PMID 40667986, 2025].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0122, EC-0124, EC-0005, QC-0038 → SUPPORTS (now human-tissue anchored).

### F1.4: STEP61 couples disinhibition to NMDAR potentiation in rodent AND human spinal cord
- **Evidence:** Ex vivo BDNF treatment of adult human spinal slices drives membrane KCC2 downregulation; loss of STEP61 couples disinhibition to NMDAR potentiation, with a male-specific pathway (STEP61–pFyn–pGluN2B) confirmed across rodent and human tissue [Dedek et al., Brain 142:1535–1546, 2019, doi:10.1093/brain/awz105; sexual dimorphism follow-up Brain 145:1124, 2022, https://academic.oup.com/brain/article/145/3/1124/6551129].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0017, EC-0122, EC-0124, EC-0005 → SUPPORTS; secondary NEW_MECHANISM (STEP61/pFyn node between disinhibition and glutamatergic sensitization).

### F1.5: Nociceptor-localized (DRG) KCC2 suppresses neuropathic pain via BDNF signaling — a peripheral arm of the cascade
- **Evidence:** In a new C7 brachial-plexus-avulsion mouse model, DRG KCC2 is decreased; DRG-specific KCC2 overexpression normalizes DRG hyperexcitability, reduces spinal dorsal-horn calcium signals, and lowers BDNF synthesis/release; avulsed human roots show ↓KCC2 with ↑BDNF/TrkB, matching rodents [Xian et al., Cell Biosci. 15:12, 2025, doi:10.1186/s13578-025-01354-5].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0017, EC-0031 (DRGX rationale_for BDNF–TrkB–KCC2 cascade) → NEW_MECHANISM (peripheral/nociceptor KCC2 as upstream regulator of BDNF; adds peripheral directionality to the cascade).

### F1.6: PV gate-keeper circuit extended to firing patterns, network decorrelation, and SK-channel tuning
- **Evidence:** Chronic pain shifts PV-neuron firing patterns, and restoring high-frequency firing attenuates allodynia [Qiu et al., PNAS 121(27):e2403777121, 2024, doi:10.1073/pnas.2403777121]; nerve injury decorrelates DH network activity via PV+ alterations [Rankin et al., Cell Rep. 43:113718, 2024, doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113718]; SK-channel/calcium-buffering tuning of PV excitability modeled and tested [Ma et al., J. Neurosci. 43:5608–5622, 2023, doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0426-23.2023]; PV circuits are diverse (inhibitory and excitatory PV populations) [Gradwell et al., Pain 163:e432, 2022, doi:10.1097/j.pain.0000000000002422].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0097 (chemogenetic/optogenetic PV rescue), EC-0025, EC-0099 → SUPPORTS; EXTENDS at mechanism level (firing-pattern restoration as therapeutic readout).

### F1.7: RARα-mediated homeostatic plasticity in PV+ neurons as a driver of disinhibition (preprint)
- **Evidence:** SNI triggers homeostatic synaptic plasticity that suppresses PV+ outputs onto afferent terminals and excitatory interneurons; RARα deletion in PV+ neurons blocks disinhibition, central sensitization and allodynia [Cao, Scherrer & Chen, bioRxiv 2022, doi:10.1101/2022.01.10.475704 — not yet peer-reviewed per retrieved record].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0097, EC-0099 → NEW_MECHANISM (RARα candidate node; flag as unreviewed).

### F1.8: SST excitatory interneurons reconfirmed as mechanical-pain gate; molecular handles identified
- **Evidence:** SOM+ T-type cells are required for mechanical pain (ablation abolishes it while sparing touch/thermal); Dyn+ inhibitory neurons gate Aβ input onto SOM+ cells [Duan et al., Cell 159:1417–1432, 2014, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2014.11.003]; SST transcriptome reveals Cacna2d1 (gabapentinoid target) among top-enriched genes [Chamessian et al., Sci Rep 8:6809, 2018, doi:10.1038/s41598-018-25110-7]; Cav3.2 in SOM+ dorsal-horn interneurons participates in mechanosensation/allodynia [Front. Cell. Neurosci. 2022, doi:10.3389/fncel.2022.875726].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0111 (SST as excitatory pain drivers), EC-0112 → SUPPORTS (Prdm12 developmental specification not directly resurfaced in this sweep).

### F1.9: Touch-evoked pain after inhibitory-interneuron loss is morphine-resistant
- **Evidence:** Silencing/ablating NPY-promoter-defined dorsal-horn inhibitory interneurons converts Aβ touch input into morphine-resistant pain-like behavior; chemogenetic silencing of GABAergic DH interneurons likewise induces morphine-resistant spontaneous nocifensive behaviors [PNAS 118(3), 2021, doi:10.1073/pnas.2021220118; Koga et al., Sci Rep 7:4739, 2017, doi:10.1038/s41598-017-04972-3].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0099 (quadruple-pronged strategy rationale) → EXTENDS (adds opioid-resistance dimension to the disinhibition paradigm; relevant to opioid-sparing rationale).

### F1.10: Microglia–BDNF–TrkB→KCC2 axis reconfirmed and extended to dynamic allodynia
- **Evidence:** In SNI mice, microglial inhibition (minocycline, preventive phase) prevents membrane-KCC2 downregulation and dynamic allodynia; exogenous BDNF reverses protection, implicating BDNF-TrkB→KCC2 in the dynamic (as well as punctate) allodynia subtype [Mol. Pain 19, 2023, doi:10.1177/17448069231185439]; consistent with the classic ATP→P2X4→BDNF microglia-to-neuron pathway [Coull 2005, see Landmarks].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0017, EC-0122, EC-0124, EC-0045 → SUPPORTS.

### F1.11: Calpain cleavage of KCC2 confirmed as injury-driven mechanism
- **Evidence:** NMDAR- and calpain-mediated proteolytic cleavage of KCC2 impairs spinal chloride homeostasis in neuropathic pain [Zhou et al., J. Biol. Chem., 2012 — cited in Lee-Hotta et al., Neurochem. Int. 128:32–38, 2019, doi:10.1016/j.neuint.2019.04.003], and calpain was independently shown to be upstream of KCC2 downregulation and motoneuron hyperexcitability after SCI [Aix-Marseille thesis 2018, https://theses.fr/2018AIXM0677.pdf].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0027 (calpain cleaves KCC2), EC-0035 → SUPPORTS.

### F1.12: IL-6/JAK2/STAT3 validated as pronociceptive glial axis — but direct STAT3→KCC2 link remains unproven in retrieved literature
- **Evidence:** IL-6/JAK2/STAT3 drives microglial/astrocytic activation and below-level pain after SCI (anti-IL-6 antibody and minocycline alleviate) [Lee et al., Exp. Neurol. 370:114576, 2023, doi:10.1016/j.expneurol.2023.114576]; separately, an autocrine neuronal IL-6 loop mediates chloride accumulation and NKCC1 phosphorylation in axotomized sensory neurons [Pieraut et al., J. Neurosci. 31:13516–13526, 2011, doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3382-11.2011].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0045 (IL-6/gp130/JAK2 downregulates KCC2 via STAT3) → EXTENDS (axis validated; the specific downstream target KCC2 vs NKCC1 needs verification — flagged as gap).

### F1.13: Microglial PNN degradation targets lamina I projection neurons, NOT PV interneurons — partial contradiction of KB framing
- **Evidence:** After nerve injury, microglia degrade perineuronal nets that selectively enwrap spinoparabrachial projection neurons in lamina I; degradation enhances projection-neuron activity and causes pain (i.e., spinal PNNs are protective) [Tansley et al., Science 377:80–86, 2022, doi:10.1126/science.abl6773]. Supraspinally, however, PNN formation around PV+ interneurons can lower pain thresholds, so PNN effects are region-dependent [PMC12004981, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12004981].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0070 (MMP-9 damages PV interneurons via PNN degradation) → CONTRADICTS (cell-type mismatch: strongest new evidence assigns pronociceptive PNN degradation to projection neurons; KB claim may need re-scoping).

### F1.14: Early KCC2 downregulation can be adaptive — temporal nuance against blanket pathogenicity
- **Evidence:** KCC2 downregulation after sciatic nerve injury transiently benefits recovery of motoneuron circuit function, i.e., early-phase downregulation enhanced motor-function recovery [Cheung et al., Sci Rep 13:7871, 2023, doi:10.1038/s41598-023-34701-y]; BDNF-TrkB effects on KCC2/GABA transmission are likewise context-dependent (upregulation reported in immature neurons or via Shc-only signaling) [Rivera 2004; citing-literature consensus, see #7 log row].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0122/EC-0124 (TrkB causes KCC2 downregulation → pain) → CONTRADICTS (temporal/context qualifier needed: pathogenic in chronic dorsal-horn pain states, adaptive early post-lesion in motor circuits).

### F1.15: Human biomarkers of spinal KCC2 function: HRDD matures, CSF-KCC2 remains disease-restricted
- **Evidence:** Rate-dependent depression of the H-reflex (HRDD) behaves as a pharmacodynamic biomarker of spinal KCC2 function; impaired in ~30–40% of painful diabetic neuropathy patients and correlates with pain phenotypes/analgesic response [Front. Mol. Neurosci. 2024, doi:10.3389/fnmol.2024.1503070; Marshall et al., Brain Commun. 5:fcad051, 2023, doi:10.1093/braincomms/fcad051]. CSF cation-chloride cotransporters have been quantified only in Rett syndrome to date [Duarte et al., PLoS ONE 8:e68851, 2013, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0068851].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0038/KCC2 dossier → EXTENDS (adds HRDD as measurable human edge-verification channel; no chronic-pain CSF-KCC2 biomarker exists yet).

## Contradictions & Contested Areas
- **EC-0070 (MMP-9 → PNN damage → PV interneurons):** Best current evidence localizes pronociceptive microglial PNN degradation to lamina I projection neurons (Tansley 2022), not PV interneurons; supraspinal PNN effects run opposite to spinal ones (region-dependent sign). Recommend re-scoping the claim.
- **Temporal sign of KCC2 downregulation:** Early post-injury downregulation aids motor recovery (Cheung 2023) and BDNF can upregulate KCC2 in some contexts (immature neurons; Shc-only TrkB signaling) — blanket "downregulation = maladaptive" edges need time-window qualifiers.
- **CLP257 mechanism of action:** Contested — one study concludes CLP257 does not directly modify KCC2 transport activity (PMC7371006); PNAS 2026 supports a clustering/trafficking mechanism. Potency numbers in QC-0040/QC-0042 should carry a MOA caveat.
- **Unverified KB quantitative cards:** ARN23746, STAB-1, BUM-9082 and the dual CLP290+bumetanide synergy figures (QC-0040/41/43/44/45) were NOT independently surfaced by this sweep's searches; verify against primary sources before promotion.
- **EC-0045 downstream target:** IL-6/JAK2/STAT3 pronociceptive role is solid, but retrieved studies tie the axis to glial activation and NKCC1 phosphorylation, not directly to STAT3-driven KCC2 transcription.

## Sweep Limitations
- Abstract/snippet-level reading for most sources; several paywalled (Nature landmarks, Science 2022, Exp Neurol 2023) — details taken from abstracts, reference lists, and open reviews.
- ARN23746, STAB-1, BUM-9082 and combined-therapy ED50 synergy figures in KB quantitative cards (QC-0040/41/42/43/44/45) were not independently found in search results; their primary papers did not surface. Treat as unverified-by-this-sweep.
- Prdm12→SST developmental specification (EC-0112) and CP-AMPAR-mediated PV excitotoxic death (EC-0025) were not directly probed/resurfaced; left untouched.
- Cao/Scherrer/Chen RARα finding is a bioRxiv preprint (2022) in retrieved form; peer-reviewed status unconfirmed.
- No chronic-pain-specific CSF-KCC2 biomarker study exists yet (only Rett syndrome); HRDD is electrophysiological, not biochemical.
- Search engine coverage bias possible for very recent (mid-2026) publications; industry pipeline status reflects the Dec 2024 review plus 2026 PNAS item.
