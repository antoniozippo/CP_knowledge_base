## Findings vs Existing Claims

### F10.1: The entire published Kalirin-7–pain literature is spinal and pronociceptive — the ACC/anxiety narrative has no retrievable primary source
- **Evidence:** Systematic PubMed enumeration (`kalirin OR KALRN "pain"`; results-by-year histogram 2013→2025, ≈10 articles). Flagship: "A role for Kalirin-7 in nociceptive sensitization via activity-dependent modulation of spinal synapses" [Nature Communications 2015, DOI 10.1038/ncomms7820] — spinal Kal7 activates Rac1, remodels dorsal-horn dendritic spines, supports NR2B-dependent sensitization in inflammatory models; i.e., Kal7 gain-of-function drives pain. Follow-ups extend this in neuropathic/postoperative/diabetic models: SGK1 mediates neuropathic pain via kalirin and downstream PSD-95-dependent NR2B phosphorylation (spinal, rat); SIRT1 attenuates neuropathic pain via CDK5–Kal7 signaling in type-2 diabetes; Kal7 contributes to T2D neuropathic pain via PSD-95/NR2B-dependent NR2B phosphorylation; spinal PKMζ regulates AMPAR trafficking and spine plasticity via Kal7; spinal Tyr1472-NR2B regulates DMT1-mediated iron accumulation and spine morphogenesis via Kal7 in fracture-associated postoperative pain (2023).
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0052 (Kal7 activates Rac1), EC-0060/EC-0101 (Rac1→PAK→LIMK→cofilin), EC-0053 (RhoG), EC-0030 (cofilin depolymerizes actin) → **SUPPORTS** (canonical, well-replicated general biology). But EC-0058/EC-0059 (CCI/SNI *induce downregulation* of Kal7) → **CONTRADICTS in sign** wherever generalized beyond an unverified ACC compartment (spinal injury models show Kal7 recruitment/upstream drive with pronociceptive function). ACC-specific claim cards EC-0054 (AAV-Kal7 rescue), EC-0061 (shRNA knockdown), EC-0062/EC-0065 (spine loss), EC-0063/EC-0066 (pain-related anxiety), EC-0064 (DBS normalization), EC-0068 (environmental enrichment), plus EC-0026/EC-0085/EC-0055 (calcineurin degradation loop), EC-0086/EC-0087 (AP-5/ketamine preserve Kal7), EC-0147 (BDNF translational upregulation of Kal7), QC-0081 (3-day onset, ≥4-week persistence) → **NOT VERIFIABLE / unsupported by any retrieved source** — consistent with their INFERRED status in the graph; recommend demotion to hypothesis nodes with zero confidence until a primary source exists.

### F10.2: Two-LTP framework in ACC reconfirmed as current standard model (post-synaptic NMDAR-LTP ↔ pain affect; pre-synaptic kainate/AC1/HCN-LTP ↔ anxiety)
- **Evidence:** Koga K, et al. Neuron 2015;85(2):377-389 — pre-LTP requires kainate receptors, AC1/PKA, expressed via HCN channels and mediates pain-related anxiety; post-LTP is NMDAR-dependent. Consolidated in Bliss TVP, Collingridge GL, Kaang BK, Zhuo M, Nat Rev Neurosci 2016;17(8):485-496 (DOI 10.1038/nrn.2016.68) and Zhuo M, Phil Trans R Soc B 2014;369:20130146. Recent extensions: pathway-specific loss of spike-timing-LTD from anterior thalamus to ACC after CCI, via exclusion of GluN2B-NMDARs from postsynaptic sites (Hogrefe et al., J Neurosci 2022;42(11):2166-2179); silent-synapse recruitment in adult ACC (Zhuo, Mol Pain 2023;19, DOI 10.1177/1744806931179011/PMID 37227022); oxytocin in ACC attenuates neuropathic pain + anxiety by inhibiting pre-LTP (Li XH et al., Cell Rep 2021); ACC potentiation in Parkinson's-disease chronic pain (Zhou Z et al., Mol Brain 2021;14:161); PKMζ maintenance of ACC LTP and chronic pain (Science 2010;330:1400-1404).
- **Relation to KB:** Dossier §3 "dissociation of emotional and sensory components" and the notion that ACC synaptic weakening accompanies anxiety → **SUPPORTS** the phenomenon-level framing (dimension dissociation is real and mechanism-backed), while showing the established molecular substrate is AC1/GluN2B/HCN/PKMζ, **not** Kalirin-7 → EXTENDS context; NB001/AC1 and oxytocin emerge as better-evidenced handles than any Kal7 intervention.

### F10.3: PSD-95–GluN2B axis verified both supraspinally (ACC) and spinally; pharmacological PSD-95 disruption matures into tolerance-free candidates
- **Evidence:** (a) ACC: PSD-95 in the ACC contributes to neuropathic pain through interdependent activation with NR2B [Li A, Huang CJ, Yao YX, Sci Rep 2022, DOI 10.1038/s41598-022-21488-7]; presynaptic/postsynaptic amplification after nerve injury involves PSD-95-NR2B interaction (Xu H et al., J Neurosci 2008;28:7445). (b) Spinal dorsal horn: CCI raises PSD-95 expression and PSD-95-NR2B coupling; intrathecal PSD-95 inhibitor NA-1 reverses thermal/mechanical hyperalgesia; Ro25-6981 lowers PSD-95 [Sci Rep 2018, DOI 10.1038/s41598-018-26209-7]. (c) Translational: ZL006 (nNOS-PSD-95 uncoupler) chronically applied causes GABAergic disinhibition and analgesic tolerance, solved by dual-target ZL006-05 (+ α2-GABA_A PAM borneol esterified) — tolerance-free analgesia in SNL rats [Li J et al., Theranostics 2021;11(12):5970-5985, DOI 10.7150/thno.58364]; perturbing PSD-95-NR2B interaction reduces central sensitization [D'Mello R et al., Mol Ther 2011]; new dorsal-horn MAGUK-associated protein (BEGAIN-type) regulating GluN2B-Y1472 and allodynia (J Pain 2026).
- **Relation to KB:** PSD-95 appears in KB only inside the Kalirin-7 dossier's scaffolding description and indirectly via EC-0125 (TrkB potentiates GluN2B-NMDAR). Findings → **EXTENDS**: PSD-95 deserves its own node independent of Kal7; EC-0125 SUPPORTED (BDNF-TrkB drives GluN2B trafficking/potentiation in rACC aversion circuits, F10.4); ZL006-05 → **NOVEL_TARGET**.

### F10.4: BDNF-TrkB in rACC is a validated pro-affective/pro-nociceptive pathway — now with microglial source and circuit specificity
- **Evidence:** (a) rACC BDNF-TrkB blockade (cyclotraxin-B) reduces bone-cancer-pain aversion; exogenous BDNF raises NR2B/pERK/pCREB, reversed by TrkB block, Ro25-6981 or U0126 [Li TT et al., Brain Res Bull 2022, DOI 10.1016/j.brainresbull.2022.07.007-class (S0361923022000934)]. Antecedents: BDNF in rACC drives spontaneous-pain aversion via NR2B (Brain Res Bull 2016) and SNI aversion via ERK (Brain Res 2017). (b) BDNF application produces long-lasting synaptic enhancement in adult ACC, blocked by the AC1 inhibitor NB001 [Mol Brain 2021, DOI 10.1186/s13041-021-00853-z] — mechanistically linking BDNF to the pre-LTP/anxiety arm. (c) Microglial P2X4R-BDNF-TrkB communication promotes ACC synaptic plasticity and hyperactivity in muscle-pain chronicity [Liang Y et al., Br J Anaesth 2025;135(4):710-722, DOI 10.1016/j.bja.2025.04.048].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0016 (BDNF activates TrkB.FL), EC-0125 (TrkB→NMDAR potentiation) → **SUPPORTS**; EC-0017/EC-0121/EC-0122 (TrkB→MAPK, →KCC2↓) → **SUPPORTS** in principle (MAPK arm directly shown in rACC aversion; KCC2 arm covered by Theme 1, not re-sourced here). EC-0147 (BDNF upregulates Kal7 translation) → **NO_SUPPORT_FOUND** (no retrieved source connects BDNF to Kalrn translation in any region). EC-0022 (TrkB-Fc sequesters BDNF), QC-0066/0067 (ANA-12 IC50 ~45 nM; 0.5 mg/kg i.p. brain-wide TrkB inhibition within 4 h) → **SUPPORTS** consistent with published ANA-12 pharmacology. New: microglia-to-neuron BDNF source in ACC → **NEW_MECHANISM** candidate edge (P2X4R→BDNF release→TrkB→ACC hyperactivity).

### F10.5: ANA-12 as tool compound revalidated; ketamine–BDNF–TrkB–spine interplay consistent
- **Evidence:** ANA-12 high-affinity non-competitive TrkB binding (~45 nM) with brain penetration at 0.5 mg/kg i.p. within hours, blocking BDNF-mediated neurite outgrowth — matches the quantitative statements in ana-12.md (Cazorla et al. 2011 lineage; corroborated by usage across the 2022/2025 BCP and muscle-pain studies above where ANA-12/cyclotraxin-B block rACC TrkB effects). The KB's QC-0068 (ANA-12 reverses ketamine's antinociception accompanied by BDNF/TrkB/synaptophysin/spine increases in diabetic neuropathy) is directionally consistent with the retrieved literature that BDNF-TrkB supports ACC/spine plasticity in chronic pain (F10.4a/b), though the specific diabetic-ketamine study was not independently retrieved.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0001, EC-0003, EC-0123, EC-0126, EC-0140 → **SUPPORTS** (tool validity); QC-0068 → **PLAUSIBLE/UNVERIFIED** (keep medium-low confidence).

### F10.6: Pain–anxiety comorbidity circuitry expanded far beyond the KB's single-region story
- **Evidence:** (a) ACC→VTA→ACC positive-feedback loop sustains neuropathic pain and emotional consequences [Nat Neurosci 2024;27:272-285, DOI 10.1038/s41593-023-01519-w]; ACC projections to NAc/VTA mediate evoked aversion [Neurobiol Dis 2020;140:104862]; ACC→PAG controls pain avoidance [Curr Biol 2022;32:2834-2847.e5]. (b) BLA→ACC pathway contributes to depression comorbidity [Nat Commun 2023]; BLA→ACC to postherpetic-neuralgia anxiety [Theranostics 2025;15]; ACC→lateral habenula controls chronic-pain-induced depression [Neuropsychopharmacology 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41386-026-02346-w]. (c) Astrocytic S100B-RAGE on VTA-projecting ACC neurons couples inflammation to anxiety [Mol Neurobiol 2025, DOI 10.1007/s12035-025-04713-y]. (d) Synergistic PV-interneuron + dopamine deficits drive ACC dysfunction [PNAS 2025, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2502558122]; EA restores PV-mediated inhibition/anxiety [Front Neurosci 2021;15:691931]. (e) Synthesis reviews: "Janus effect of the ACC" (Neurosci Biobehav Rev 2023) and "The ACC as an integrative hub: mechanisms underlying pain and anxiety comorbidity" (Neurosci Biobehav Rev 2026, S014976342600103X); IBRO 2025 systematic review timing anxiety 1 d–4 wk post-CFA with ACC/amygdala excitability mechanisms.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0063/EC-0066 (pain-related anxiety as ACC-Kal7-dependent) → the *phenomenon* is **SUPPORTED**, but attribution shifts to circuits (VTA/LHb/BLA/PAG loops) and molecules (AC1, GluN2B, RAGE, PV/DA, P2X4R-BDNF) with primary evidence → **EXTENDS** + multiple **NEW_MECHANISM** edges below.

### F10.7: Structural plasticity in ACC: biphasic dynamics (early LTP gain, late spine loss) and exercise reversal — phenomenon-level support without Kalirin-7
- **Evidence:** The 2025/26 ACC neuroplasticity review states early LTP enhancement coexists with later synaptic loss in ACC chronicity (PMC13080107); voluntary running improves ACC synaptic degeneration in knee-osteoarthritis mice [Mol Brain 2025, DOI 10.1186/s13041-025-01207-9]; increasing APC/C^Cdh1 activity in ACC alleviates allodynia with spine remodeling [Mol Pain 2015, DOI 10.1186/s12990-015-0058-6]; AMPAR subunit switching (GluA2 internalization → CP-AMPAR insertion) documented in ACC layers II/III and V after nerve injury [J Anesth 2026 review, DOI 10.1007/s00540-026-03729-5].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0062/EC-0065 (ACC L2/3 spine loss) → phenomenon **SUPPORTS** (plausible and partially documented), molecular attribution to Kal7 **UNVERIFIED**; EC-0068 (environmental enrichment boosts ACC Kal7) → the EE/exercise→ACC-plasticity direction gains indirect **SUPPORTS** (running paper), the Kal7 mediator remains unverified → **EXTENDS** with corrected mechanism label.

### F10.8: CaMKII–NR2B–AMPAR trafficking cascade verified in pain (and the neuropsin negative result)
- **Evidence:** Spinal phosphorylation cascade Tyr1472-NR2B ← Thr286-CaMKII → Ser831-GluR1 in neuropathic pain (Katano et al., Neuropharmacology 2011; surfaced via 2026 review reference lists); pCaMKII binds/stabilizes postsynaptic NR2B in ACC in chronic pancreatitis hyperalgesia+anxiety model [Neurosci Bull 2021, DOI 10.1007/s12264-021-00800-x]; canonical CaMKII-driven GluA1-PDZ-dependent AMPAR delivery during LTP (Hayashi-type mechanism cited in ACC mTOR paper); AMPAR-trafficking-in-pain synthesis including human PET imaging ([11C]K-2) [J Anesth 2026]. **Negative result:** neuropsin (KLK8) — no connection to chronic pain or ACC retrievable; its validated role is hippocampal early-phase LTP via N-cadherin cleavage (ScienceDirect topic overview). Note: no KB claim actually references neuropsin (grep-verified), so this closes the sweep question without impact on the graph.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0088 (NMDAR Ca²⁺ influx activates CaMKII as plasticity marker) → **SUPPORTS**; EC-0020 (BDNF promotes plasticity-protein biomarker panel) → **SUPPORTS** generically.

### F10.9: NSC23766 tool claims carry significant off-target caveats
- **Evidence:** NSC23766 is a competitive antagonist at muscarinic receptors within the same concentration range used for Rac1 inhibition [J Pharmacol Exp Ther 2013, PMID 23887096, DOI 10.1124/jpet.113.207266] and suppresses NMDAR-CREB signaling by targeting NMDAR function [J Neurosci 2014;34(42):14006]. Published CNS microinjection uses are in NAc (addiction memory, Theranostics 2019) — not ACC pain. No study found injecting NSC23766 into ACC to modulate pain or anxiety.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0102 (NSC23766 inhibits Rac1) → **SUPPORTS** as pharmacology; the dossier's ACC-microinjection behavioral interpretation (mimics Kal7 loss, induces anxiety) → **NOT_VERIFIABLE**, and the off-target profile means even a future positive result would be confounded (muscarinic antagonism alone alters ACC excitability) → flag.

### F10.10: TNFα-to-spine-loss link in ACC remains indirect
- **Evidence:** ACC astrocyte pro-inflammatory transformation releases TNF-α/IL-6, disrupts E/I balance and glutamate homeostasis contributing to hyperexcitability and comorbid anxiety-like behavior (2025/26 ACC review, PMC13080107, citing Wei 2024, Zhou Q 2025); astrocyte activation in ACC contributes to affective component of pain (Brain Res Bull 2011/2012 lineage cited in RAGE paper). Direct TNFα→dendritic-spine-elimination evidence in ACC was not retrieved; spinal TNFα→AMPAR-trafficking effects are classical.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0167 (TNFα contributes to ACC L2/3 spine loss) → **PARTIAL/EXTENDS**: cytokine→ACC plasticity supported at network level; specific spine-morphology endpoint unverified → keep low-medium confidence.

## Contradictions & Contested Areas
1. **ACC Kalirin-7 narrative (dossier core) vs absent literature.** All ACC-specific claim cards (EC-0054, EC-0061–EC-0068, EC-0026/EC-0055/EC-0085–EC-0087, EC-0147, EC-0157/EC-0160–EC-0163 as ACC-contextualized, QC-0081) lack retrievable primary support; the only real Kal7-pain data invert the sign (spinal, pronociceptive). Treat the 17 flagged inferred edges accordingly; recommend graph surgery separating "general Kal7 biology (supported)" from "Kal7-in-ACC-pain (unsupported hypothesis)".
2. **Sign inversion risk for therapy.** KB proposes restoring Kal7 to relieve pain-affect; if Kal7 is pronociceptive at spinal level, systemic or broad CNS Kal7 enhancement could worsen sensory sensitization — reinforcing the dossier's own regional-heterogeneity caution, but with stronger empirical teeth.
3. **NSC23766 interpretability.** Muscarinic antagonism (JPET 2013) + NMDAR/CREB suppression (J Neurosci 2014) make any ACC-behavioral result non-interpretable as Rac1-specific.
4. **BDNF-in-ACC valence contested.** Dominant finding: pronociceptive/pro-affective via TrkB-NR2B-ERK (rACC aversion studies; P2X4R-BDNF microglia). Counter-note in psilocybin-review literature: cortical BDNF described as facilitatory with anxiolytic-like associations, and enhancing pro-BDNF signalling reduced CUMS-induced anxiety — context-, ligand- (pro- vs mature-) and receptor-complex-dependent. EC-0021's dual-role rationale is vindicated.
5. **What ACC plasticity *means*.** The 2025/26 ACC review argues ACC changes may be substrates of aversive learning/threat prediction rather than direct nociceptive amplification; KB should avoid over-strong "ACC drives pain" phrasing for edges derived from aversion assays.
6. **Temporal structure.** Early LTP/hyperactivity vs late spine loss coexist in different windows (review; tLTD paper); static edges like "nerve injury → ACC spine loss" need time-stamped qualifiers.
