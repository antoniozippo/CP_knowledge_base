## Findings vs Existing Claims

### F4.1: HIF-1α-driven glycolytic shift in microglia/macrophages is a validated, upstream-regulated pronociceptive hub
- **Evidence:** Multiple 2021–2026 papers converge on immune-cell aerobic glycolysis driving NP: PRMT6 methyltransferase activity stabilizes HIF-1α, increasing glycolysis and neuroinflammation; PRMT6 deficiency/inhibition alleviates NP [Pain Research Forum entry, https://painresearchforum.org/paper/prmt6-deficiency-or-inhibition-alleviates-neuropathic-pain-by-decreasing-glycolysis-and-inflammation-in-microglia]. HK2 is induced in spinal microglia AND DRG macrophages after nerve injury; myeloid deletion of Hk2 alleviates early-phase mechanical pain, with peripheral monocytes/DRG macrophages dominating over spinal microglia [Wang et al., Glia 2023, PMID 37909251, doi:10.1002/glia.24482]. Lyn kinase enhances microglial glycolysis via IRF5 nuclear translocation [IASP Papers of the Week, https://www.iasp-pain.org/publications/pain-research-forum/papers-of-the-week/paper/lyn-mediated-glycolysis-enhancement-of-microglia-contributes-to-neuropathic-pain-through-facilitating-irf5-nuclear-translocation-in-spinal-dorsal-horn]. In neurons, FGF4 drives HIF-1α-mediated glycolysis, exacerbating excitability; HIF-1α inhibition relieved CCI pain [Wu/Chen, J Headache Pain 2026, doi:10.1186/s10194-026-02362-7].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0024 (BDNF→HIF1α glycolytic shift), EC-0039 (HIF1α transcriptional battery), QC-0085 (2-DG) → SUPPORTS; upstream regulators (PRMT6, Lyn-IRF5, HK2, FGF4) → NEW_MECHANISM nodes above the existing HIF1α node. Caveat: cell-type emphasis extends beyond microglia to DRG macrophages and neurons.

### F4.2: Lactate–histone lactylation axis independently confirmed as analgesic target; exact FX11 quantitative claim still unverified
- **Evidence:** Peripheral nerve injury raises H3K18 lactylation in DRG; H3K18la activates Igf2bp2 transcription, stabilizing Cct2 mRNA; 2-DG or Igf2bp2 knockdown attenuates hypersensitivity, while sodium lactate injected into uninjured DRG induces pain-like behavior [J Neurosci 2026;46(1), PMID 41260921]. Neuronal ACVR1 activation in dorsal horn drives glycolysis/lactate/H3K18la and NLRP3 pyroptosis; delayed ACVR1 blockade (LDN-193189) or oxamate reverses established allodynia [Neurobiol Dis 2026;224:107397, doi:10.1016/j.nbd.2026.107397]. Amphiregulin enhances sensory-neuron glycolysis stimulating histone lactylation [Sci Signal 2025;18(891):eadr9397]. Review consolidates the Warburg-shift → lactylation → pro-pain gene program loop in microglia [Mol Neurobiol 2026, doi:10.1007/s12035-026-05989-4].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0072 (LDHA/FX11 intrathecal 10 µg eliminates H3K18la at pain promoters, reverses allodynia within 72 h, "2024") → SUPPORTS in principle (lactylation axis now multiply replicated) but the specific FX11 dose/effect parameters were NOT retrieved verbatim in this sweep → keep citation_status=UNVERIFIED pending source check; QC-0085 (2-DG) → SUPPORTS (independent replication of glycolysis-blockade analgesia in CCI/SNI-type models).

### F4.3: PX-478 and YC-1 remain tool compounds; no pain-efficacy program exists — and YC-1 carries a stroke-safety red flag
- **Evidence:** PX-478 suppresses HIF-1α at multiple levels (mRNA reduction, translation inhibition, protein destabilization) and blocks hypoxia-induced VEGF [Koh et al., Mol Cancer Ther 2008;7:90–100; NCI Drug Dictionary]; development stopped at Phase I oncology (neutropenia dose-limiting) with no neurological indication. YC-1 potently inhibits HIF-1 transcriptional activity (HeLa HRE IC50 ≈ 1.2 µM) [Selleck compilation] and ameliorated allergic airway inflammation in rats [Eur J Pharmacol 2016], BUT in transient focal ischemia YC-1 significantly increased mortality and infarct volume [PMC3218033, 2011]. As of 2023, no HIF-1α inhibitor has reached clinical practice [Bioorg Chem review 2023, pii S0045206823000603]. Indirect analgesia support: tetrahydropalmatine alleviates osteoarthritis pain by suppressing KDM4A/MDM2/HIF-1α-mediated M1 macrophage polarization [PMID 41857767, 2026].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0040 (PX-478 inhibits HIF1α translation) → SUPPORTS mechanistically; EC-0038 (YC-1 destabilizes HIF1α) → SUPPORTS + safety caveat (NEW_MECHANISM/risk edge: HIF inhibition harmful in ischemic CNS); EC-0042 (rationale: isoform specificity/systemic risks) → SUPPORTS, now with concrete counter-examples.

### F4.4: Landmarks reconfirmed — HIF1α→VEGFA transcription and inflammatory (normoxic) HIF stabilization
- **Evidence:** Cramer et al. showed myeloid HIF-1α is essential for inflammation-driven glycolysis, motility and VEGF expression in vivo [Cell 2003;112:645–657, doi:10.1016/S0092-8674(03)00154-5]. Palazon et al. systematized that hypoxia and TLR/NF-κB signaling converge on HIF-1α mRNA induction in phagocytes (normoxic stabilization), and that myeloid HIF-1α drives VEGF; also documents Ang-2 sensitization of endothelium to TNF-α era literature and Tie2-expressing monocytes [Immunity 2014;41:518–528, doi:10.1016/j.immuni.2014.09.008]. Hypoxia→VEGF founding observation reconfirmed [Shweiki et al., Nature 1992;359:843–845].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0041 (HIF1α transcribes VEGFA), EC-0116 (TNF/NF-kB normoxic HIF stabilization), EC-0164 (TNF↔VEGFA mutual upregulation), EC-0073 (MMP-9↔HIF1α; see F4.8) → SUPPORTS. HIF-1α/MMP-9 co-drive spinal central sensitization reported [Eur J Pharmacol 2023, pii S0014299923003692] → EXTENDS EC-0073 (axis-level rather than strict MMP9→HIF1α causality).

### F4.5: BSCB disruption in neuropathic pain — solid preclinical base, heterogeneous across models
- **Evidence:** Remote peripheral nerve injury opens the BSCB within 24–48 h (Evans blue/HRP), prevented by local lidocaine and mimicked by selective C-fiber (TRPV1+) stimulation [Beggs et al., Mol Pain 2010;6:74, doi:10.1186/1744-8069-6-74]; persistent vascular dysfunction and endoneurial hypoxia follow PNI [J Neurosci 2015;35:3346–61]. CXCL10/CXCR3 increases BSCB permeability enabling immune-cell migration in CCI [Front Immunol 2020;11:477]. Endoglin downregulation peaks day 3 post-SNI coinciding with maximal endothelial-gap formation (TEM/Evans blue); recombinant endoglin restores BSCB via TGF-β/Smad2 and alleviates pain [Front Mol Neurosci 2025;18:1603619]. In PNL, MCP-1/IL-1β open the barrier while IL-10/TGF-β1 close it through ZO-1/occludin reorganization with monocyte recruitment [summarized in Montague-Cardoso & Malcangio, Pain Rep 2021, PMC8108584]. Same review stresses inconsistency: e.g., convincing tracer leakage in some models but lineage-tracing showing no parenchymal infiltration after SNI.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0013 (BSCB disruption), EC-0014 (RvD1/LXA4 resealing), EC-0071 (MMP-9 degrades occludin/claudin-5/ZO-1), EC-0115 (TNF downregulates TJs), EC-0012 (repair-vs-delivery rationale) → SUPPORTS overall; strength qualifier needed on EC-0013 (model-dependent, often transient) → annotate EXTENDS with temporal-dynamics nuance. EC-0014 remains only partially corroborated (SPM-BSCB direct data thin in retrieved results) → downgrade expectation, citation_status stays UNVERIFIED.

### F4.6: Angiopoietin-1/Tie2 stabilization vs Ang-2 antagonism — strongly confirmed, with a new drugable upstream node (neutrophil elastase)
- **Evidence:** Ang-1 activates Tie2 promoting quiescence/junction tightening [Koh, Trends Mol Med 2013;19:31–39]; IV Ang-1 (+αvβ3 peptide) after contusion SCI reduced pathological BSCB permeability at 72 h and rescued white matter/locomotion [Brain 2010;133:1026–1042]; sustained VEGF+Ang-1 expression improves BSCB integrity [J Neurotrauma 2010;27:2067–2076]; Ang-1 regulates permeability/junctions in spinal-cord microvascular endothelial cells via Akt [PMID 25149679, 2014]. Ang-2 antagonizes Tie2 and sensitizes ECs to TNF-α [Nat Med 2006;12:235]. After SCI, neutrophil elastase rises, Ang-1 falls and Ang-2 rises; sivelestat restored Ang-1 (AKT pathway), prevented tight-junction degradation, and attenuated neuropathic pain [Acta Neuropathol Commun 2018, doi:10.1186/s40478-018-0576-3].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0006 (Ang-1 activates Tie2), EC-0007 (Ang-2 antagonism in inflammation), EC-0008 (Ang-2 synergizes VEGFA), EC-0015 (Ang-1 stabilizes BBB/BSCB) → SUPPORTS; NEW_MECHANISM/NOVEL_TARGET: Neutrophil-elastase→Ang-1 degradation edge + sivelestat repurposing candidate. EC-0159 (RvD1/LXA4 complementary to Ang-1) → plausible but untested combination; no direct study retrieved → UNVERIFIED.

### F4.7: VEGFA dosage/duality and isoform splicing — KB's "crucial for repair, drives pain when excessive" framing now has receptor-resolved mechanics
- **Evidence:** Endogenous VEGFA is upregulated in injured peripheral nerve, contributing to angiogenesis and prolonged pain [J Neurochem 2013, doi:10.1111/jnc.12614]; VEGFA-pAKT-TRPV1 colocalization in SNI, reversed by soluble VEGFR1 (sFlt-1) trap [Mol Pain 2022, doi:10.1177/17448069221094528]. Isoform level: VEGF-Axxxa (SRPK1/SRSF1-dependent splicing) pro-nociceptive via VEGFR2/TRPV1/TRPA1; VEGF-Axxxb (e.g., VEGF165b) anti-nociceptive, elevating thresholds and reversing neuropathic pain [Beazley-Long Am J Pathol 2013;183:918–929; Hulse, Channels 2017, PMC5355219; diabetic rescue PMC6209758, 2018]. Receptor level: VEGFR1 blockade (D16F7) kills CINP signaling while VEGFR2 mediates neuroprotection — VEGF165b protected oxaliplatin-treated organotypic cord slices via VEGFR2 yet raised SP/CGRP release via VEGFR1 [Brain Sci companion papers 2025, pii S1878747925000108 & S1878747925000121]; anti-VEGFR1 mAb therapeutic in CINP [J Exp Clin Cancer Res 2021, doi:10.1186/s13046-021-02127-x]. Central VEGF-A contributes to trigeminal NP [PMC6719481, 2019].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0130 (repair-vs-pain duality), EC-0011 (bevacizumab sequesters VEGFA) → SUPPORTS and refines (isoform/receptor-selective strategies supersede blunt sequestration). EC-0129 (excess VEGFA drives neuroma formation) → PARTIAL/EXTENDS: VEGFA excess clearly drives aberrant angiogenesis and prolonged pain in injured nerve, but a direct VEGFA→painful-neuoma causal study was not retrieved; 2026 neuroma work implicates axonal phenotypic shift without testing VEGF [PMID 42582843]. EC-0168 (VEGFR2→BDNF-TrkB→KCC2↓, INFERRED) → CONTESTED (see Contradictions).

### F4.8: Barrier-tight junction biology extended to the PNS compartment (claudin-12, perineurium)
- **Evidence:** Patients with painful polyneuropathy selectively lose Schwann-cell claudin-12; global Cldn12-KO causes perineurial/myelin barrier breakdown and exaggerated mechanical hypersensitivity via SHH-dependent TJ regulation [bioRxiv 2019, doi:10.1101/768267]. Transient perineurial opening (claudin-1/-5, occludin downregulation) is exploitable for analgesic delivery [PNAS 2012, doi:10.1073/pnas.1120800109] — mirrors KB's BBB repair-vs-drug-delivery tension at the blood-nerve barrier.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0071, EC-0115, EC-0012 → SUPPORTS/EXTENDS (new compartment: BNB/perineurium; new molecule claudin-12). Data-quality flags on EC-0118/EC-0119 predicates (see Contradictions).

### F4.9: HIF-1α is bidirectional — protective contexts qualify any "block HIF1α = analgesia" reading
- **Evidence:** HIF-1α ameliorates diabetic neuropathic pain via Parkin-mediated mitophagy in spinal cord [PMID 36017378, 2022]; hypoxic HIF-1α activation in DRG neurons suppresses ferroptosis and promotes nerve recovery with BDNF/NGF upregulation [PMID 38291291, 2024]; YC-1 worsens stroke outcome (F4.3); endogenous HIF-1α/PHD biology underpins reparative responses after PNI [J Neurosci 2015;35:3346 context].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0042 (rationale: systemic risks) → SUPPORTS with strengthened justification; simultaneously CONTRADICTS any simplified "HIF1α uniformly pronociceptive" interpretation elsewhere in hif1a.md — recommend adding a context/phase qualifier (acute-inflammatory vs regenerative phases; cell type).

### F4.10: Glycolytic-shift → Ang-2 inference chain (EC-0149/EC-0150) remains unsupported in pain literature
- **Evidence:** No retrieved 2021–2026 study links HIF1α transcriptionally to ANGPT2, or microglial glycolysis to Ang-2 production, in neuropathic-pain models; Ang-2 regulation retrieved here is neutrophil-elastase/inflammation-linked instead (F4.6). General angiogenesis literature treats hypoxia→Ang-2 as established but pain-context data are absent from this sweep's results.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0149, EC-0150 (both INFERRED, P1) → NOVEL/unverified; recommend keeping sign but marking evidence_level=speculative until a primary source is attached.

## Contradictions & Contested Areas
1. **HIF-1α polarity:** Pronociceptive in microglial/neuronal glycolysis programs (F4.1) yet protective via mitophagy in diabetic NP and via ferroptosis suppression in DRG regeneration (F4.9); YC-1 deleterious in stroke. KB should carry phase/cell-type qualifiers rather than a uniform sign.
2. **VEGFR2 valence:** KB EC-0168 infers VEGFR2→BDNF-TrkB→KCC2↓ (pronociceptive); 2025 organotypic-cord data show VEGFR2 signaling is neuroprotective (DC101 blockade toxic; VEGF165b protection VEGFR2-dependent), while pronociception segregates to VEGFR1. Context (chemotherapy neurotoxicity vs nerve-injury models) likely explains divergence — treat EC-0168 as contested.
3. **Isoform simplicity vs receptor pharmacology:** VEGF165b is "anti-nociceptive" in diabetes/SNI models, yet in the oxaliplatin slice model VEGF165b increased SP/CGRP release through VEGFR1 — isoform effects are receptor-context dependent.
4. **BSCB causality heterogeneity:** Tracer leakage is reproducible in CCI/PNL/SCI, but SNI shows weak infiltration evidence (lineage tracing negative) and leaks are often transient (peak 24–48 h, resolved ~day 7–14) — EC-0013 needs temporal qualification.
5. **KB internal data-quality flags (not literature contradictions):** EC-0118 ("Tight Junction Proteins degrades CypA-MMP-9") and EC-0119 ("Tight Junction Proteins downregulates VEGFR2"), plus EC-0023 ("BDNF stimulates_microglial_release_of VEGFR2") have reversed/nonsensical predicates; underlying biology (MMP-9 degrades TJs; BDNF couples to glial VEGF/VEGFR biology) is supported, so fix direction/source rather than delete.

## Post-Round Adjudications (2026-08-26)

### A4.1: BDNF supports barrier integrity — direction resolved (was graph AMBIGUOUS edge `BDNF--[regulates]-->BBB Integrity`)
- **Evidence:** Hyperoside mitigates BBB damage in bacterial meningitis via the miR-155/BDNF pathway: hyperoside downregulates miR-155, upregulates BDNF in LPS-injured hCMEC/D3 cells, increasing TEER and tight-junction proteins (ZO-1, Claudin-5, AQP4) and reducing permeability in rats; causality established because miR-155 mimic or **sh-BDNF knockdown partially reversed** the protective effects on TEER, tight-junction expression, and cytokine levels [Zhang, Zhao & Kuang, 2025, doi:10.1159/000547276]. Convergent support: BDNF supports pericyte and vascular homeostasis — conditional TrkB deletion in pericytes/SMCs increases vascular permeability, and BDNF induces PDGF-B/Akt/Erk signaling in pericytes [TrkB-pericyte aging study, springermedizin 51781984]; cerebral endothelium itself secretes mBDNF with luminal autocrine TrkB signaling [doi:10.1177/0271678X18766772].
