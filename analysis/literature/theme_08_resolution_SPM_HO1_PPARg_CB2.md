## Findings vs Existing Claims

### F8.1: SPM "resolution pharmacology" is now a consolidated framework for chronic pain
- **Evidence:** Ji's Annual Review codifies resolvins/protectins/maresins as endogenous omega-3-derived mediators with broad safety profiles acting in preclinical and clinical models of pain and itch, with distinct pathogenesis-vs-resolution mechanisms [Ji RR 2023, Annu Rev Pharmacol Toxicol 63:273-293, doi:10.1146/annurev-pharmtox-051921-084047]. Recent mechanistic reviews extend this to neuropathic, cancer, postoperative and spontaneous pain via cytokine suppression, TRP-channel modulation and neuro-immune/glial regulation [Chen Y et al. 2025, Front Immunol 16:1634724, doi:10.3389/fimmu.2025.1634724]; impaired SPM machinery framed as core event in neuropathic pain onset [Leuti A et al. 2021, Front Pharmacol 12:717993, doi:10.3389/fphar.2021.717993].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0014, EC-0159 → **SUPPORTS** (the pro-resolving role of RvD1/LXA4 is mainstream; barrier-resealing framing is a specific instance of the general resolution program). EC-0159 remains **INFERRED** — no head-to-head SPM-vs-Ang-1 comparison surfaced.

### F8.2: RvD1 mechanism map expanded — ALX/FPR2–NLRP3/ERK and a BDNF/TrkB microglial brake (cross-theme bridge)
- **Evidence:** RvD1 alleviates mechanical allodynia via ALX/FPR2-targeted NLRP3/ERK signaling in a neuropathic pain model [Wang YH et al. 2022, Neuroscience 494:12-24, doi:10.1016/j.neuroscience.2022.04.019]. RvD1 accelerates resolution of neuroinflammation by inhibiting microglial activation through the BDNF/TrkB signaling pathway [Eur J Med Res 2025;30:189, doi:10.1186/s40001-025-02424-7]. RvD2 inhibits ATP currents in rat primary sensory neurons [Hao JW et al. 2024, J Neurochem 168:3715-26, doi:10.1111/jnc.16009]. Spinal cord stimulation raises central RvD1 levels and attenuates allodynia in SNI rats [Zhang T et al. 2021, cited in Ji 2023 review].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0014 → **SUPPORTS**. The RvD1→BDNF/TrkB(microglia) finding is a **NEW_MECHANISM**: it directly connects the resolution module to the KB's BDNF–TrkB–KCC2 cascade (Theme 1, ana-12.md) — SPMs act as endogenous antagonists of the same microglial TrkB axis that ANA-12 blocks pharmacologically.

### F8.3: SPM family breadth in neuropathic-relevant models keeps growing
- **Evidence:** Aspirin-triggered lipoxin A4 reduces neuropathic pain and anxiety-like behaviours in diabetic rats, with antinociceptive enhancement by cannabinoid receptor agonists [Ferreira CHA et al. 2025, Eur J Pharmacol 989]. Protectin D1 ameliorates lumbar disc herniation pain through SIRT1-mediated CGRP signaling [Zhu YC et al. 2024, Mol Pain 20:17448069241232349, doi:10.1177/17448069241232349]. NPD1 relieves neuropathic pain after peripheral nerve injury [Tian Y et al. 2024, Pain Res Manage 2024:1109287]. Maresin 1 attenuates osteoarthritis pain [Shih YV et al. 2024, Osteoarthritis Cartilage 33:341-50].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0014/EC-0159 → **EXTENDS** (family-level generality beyond RvD1/LXA4). The AT-LXA4×cannabinoid synergy and PD1→SIRT1→CGRP links create candidate edges into Theme 7 (CGRP) and the CB2 dossier.

### F8.4: Human SPM therapeutics remain early-stage; supplements ≠ resolved SPMs
- **Evidence:** Open-label SPM-enriched marine lipid fraction (standardized to 17-HDHA/18-HEPE) improved quality of life and pain in chronic-pain adults over 4 weeks [Callan N et al. 2020, J Transl Med, PMID 33087142, NCT02683850]; authors explicitly call for placebo-controlled trials. A subsequent randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (GAUDI) tested an SPM-enriched oil in symptomatic knee osteoarthritis [Möller I et al. 2023, J Transl Med 21:423, PMID 37386594]. In healthy volunteers, circulating 17-HDHA (but not RvD1/D2/D3/D5/E1) associated with higher heat-pain thresholds; a high-vs-low omega-3 RCT in RA showed no advantage of high-dose supplementation [Sci Rep 2017, doi:10.1038/s41598-017-09516-3]. Serhan positions stereochemistry-defined SPMs for imminent human trials [Serhan CN et al. 2022, Semin Immunol 59:101597, doi:10.1016/j.smim.2022.101597].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0014 → **EXTENDS** (clinical-status context: no approved resolvin drug; precursor-supplementation evidence is weak/null). No KB claim asserts clinical efficacy, so no contradiction.

### F8.5: HO-1/CO analgesia reconfirmed and extended to gabapentinoid synergy, mood comorbidities, and CNS antioxidant signaling
- **Evidence:** CORM-2/CoPP reduce allodynia/hyperalgesia after sciatic injury by inhibiting nitric oxide synthesis and microglial activation [Hervera A et al. 2012, PLoS One 7:e43693]; CORM-2/CORM-3/CoPP enhance μ-opioid receptor expression/effect during neuropathic pain [Hervera A et al. 2013, Anesthesiology 118:1180-97]; spinal HO-1 upregulation (CORM-2 10 mg/kg/d; CoPP 5 mg/kg/d; lentiviral LV-HO-1) suppresses SNL pain partly via microglial inhibition [Pain Med 2016;17:220, doi:10.1111/pme.12906]. CoPP or CORM-2 enhances gabapentin/pregabalin antinociception, reducing M1 microglia and TNFα (M2 markers unchanged) [PAIN Reports 2018, doi:10.1097/PR9.0000000000000677]. New HO-1 inducers (1m/1a/1b) and DMF relieve nerve-injury pain plus anxiety/depressive-like behaviors, lowering NLRP3 and raising Nrf2/HO-1/SOD1 in DRG and amygdala [Suárez-Rojas I et al. 2023, Antioxidants 12:1859, doi:10.3390/antiox12101859]. CORM-2/CoPP normalize p-Akt/BAX and boost NQO1 in PAG/AMG/VHPC/MSA during persistent inflammatory pain [Life Sci 2022, pii S0361923022001952].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0036 (COHb >10-15% toxicity ceiling) → **SUPPORTS**: the entire CORM field is premised on controlled local CO delivery precisely because systemic CO is toxic [Motterlini & Otterbein 2010, Nat Rev Drug Discov 9:728-43; Ling K et al. 2017, J Med Chem miniperspective, doi:10.1021/acs.jmedchem.6b01153]. No new claim-level contradiction; isoform dichotomy (HO-1 anti- vs HO-2 pronociceptive) repeatedly reconfirmed [PLoS One 2012].

### F8.6: PPARγ agonism — pioglitazone anti-microglial analgesia strengthened; safety caveats sharpened
- **Evidence:** Pioglitazone rapidly reduces neuropathic pain behavior (non-classical, non-transcriptional kinetics discussed) [PMC4329091]; repeated PPARγ agonism reduces pain with reduced spinal glial activation [same source]. Intrathecal pioglitazone alleviates NP via microglial activation inhibition, mitochondrial protection and reduced oxidative stress [J Guangxi Med Univ 2024, doi:10.16190/j.cnki.45-1211/r.2024.02.010]. Pioglitazone repurposed in NMOSD model: PPARγ-dependent reduction of microglial neuroinflammation, IL-1β/IL-6/TNFα, and serum sTREM2 (reversed by GW9662) [Acta Neuropathol Commun 2025, doi:10.1186/s40478-025-02182-x]; similar M1/M2 repolarization after subarachnoid hemorrhage [Mol Neurobiol 2024, doi:10.1007/s12035-024-04664-w]; pioglitazone alleviates hemorrhage-induced thalamic pain [Pain Research Forum paper page]. Class review flags TZD liabilities: cardiotoxicity, weight gain, fluid retention (rosiglitazone especially) [Trends Pharmacol Sci 2024, pii S0165614723002560].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0048 → **SUPPORTS** (pioglitazone/TZDs alleviate allodynia/hyperalgesia across models) and **EXTENDS** (mechanistic depth: sTREM2 biomarker, M1/M2, mitochondrial axis). QC-0049 (15d-PGJ2 intrathecal in CCI) → **SUPPORTS** at class level (no new 15d-PGJ2-specific study surfaced; endogenous-agonist framing consistent with reviews above). Note: QC-0048 lists cannabidiol as PPARγ-related agonist — consistent with the CB2/PPARγ crosstalk literature but not directly retested here.

### F8.7: Nrf2 axis — a missing upstream hub in the KB (DMF as clinically-approved probe)
- **Evidence:** Oral DMF (FDA-approved for MS) reduces peripheral neuropathic pain in rodents via NFE2L2 antioxidant signaling [Li J, Ma J, Lacagnina MJ, … Grace PM 2020, Anesthesiology 132:343-356, PMID 31939850, doi:10.1097/ALN.0000000000003077]. Pretreatment with DMF prevents long-term allodynia and pain comorbidities via Nrf2 before nerve injury in rats [Le ACT et al. 2025, Brain Behav Immun 128:725-736, doi:10.1016/j.bbi.2025.05.003]. NRF2 activation reported to *resolve* neuropathic pain via dual actions in sensory neurons [ScienceDirect 2026, pii S1525001625010305]. DMF attenuates osteoarthritis pain via Nrf2-mediated mitochondrial biogenesis [Gao SJ et al. 2022, Mol Pain 18:17448069221124920] and paclitaxel-induced NP in rats [Singh J et al. 2022, Cureus 14:e28818]. Caveat: DMF also acts Nrf2-independently (HCAR2/β-hydroxybutyrate receptor, NF-κB, AMPK-Sirt1) [Antioxidants 2024 review, doi:10.3390/antiox13121527; Chen H et al. 2014, J Clin Invest 124:2188-92].
- **Relation to KB:** No Nrf2-bearing claims exist in claims.json despite HO-1_CO.md being in scope → **NEW_MECHANISM** (Keap1–Nrf2–HO-1 cascade upstream of the KB's HO-1 node) and **NOVEL_TARGET** (Nrf2 activators, incl. repurposable DMF; prevention-of-chronification angle maps onto EC-0120's acute-to-chronic protocol).

### F8.8: CB2 — strongest preclinical package in this theme, but human translation still failing/slow
- **Evidence:** Source claim reproduced: LY2828360 (3 mg/kg/day i.p. × 12 d) suppressed paclitaxel-induced neuropathic pain in WT mice without tolerance, absent in CB2-KO; described as slowly-signaling, G-protein-biased agonist [Lin X, Mackie K, Hohmann AG et al. 2018, PMID 29192123]. Mechanistic locus refined to CB2 receptors on primary sensory neurons (paclitaxel model; sex-dimorphic morphine-tolerance sparing) [Guenther KG et al. 2024, Biomed Pharmacother 176:116879, PMID 38850666]. In rats, LY2828360 suppresses evoked AND spontaneous (CPP-assessed) neuropathic pain, gabapentin-comparable, attenuating morphine tolerance/reward [Guenther KG et al. 2025, Neuropharmacology 265:110257, PMID 39644993]. Novel modality: first CB2-specific therapeutic antibody agonists effective in chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy models [PMC12694584, 2025], which simultaneously documents why small-molecule CB2 programs stalled (off-targets, rapid clearance, inconsistent human trials). Clinical pipeline: CNTX-6016 (Boehringer Ingelheim/Centrextion), oral CB2 agonist, completed Phase I in neuropathic pain (NCT04857957, 2023; still Phase I per AdisInsight, May 2025).
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0008 → **SUPPORTS** (exact match) and **EXTENDS** (cellular locus; spontaneous-pain dimension; opioid-sparing). QC-0007 (≈50% cognitive impairment statistic) → **NOT ASSESSED** (no search targeted it). Translation caveat logged under Contradictions.

### F8.9: S1P/S1PR1 modulation — analgesic efficacy in CNS-autoimmunity and chemo-models; safety profile as claimed
- **Evidence:** Fingolimod reduces central NP behaviors in MOG35-55 EAE mice dose-dependently and reversibly, via S1PR1-dependent inhibition of dorsal-horn central sensitization (blocked by W146; mimicked by SEW2871; reduced glutamate-evoked Ca²⁺, pERK, GFAP/Iba1) [Doolen S, … Taylor BK 2018, Pain 159:224-238, doi:10.1097/j.pain.0000000000001106]. Development AND maintenance of paclitaxel-induced NP require S1PR1 activation [J Biol Chem 2014, doi:10.1016/j.jbc (S0021-9258(20)47622-7)]. Ceramide→S1P rheostat proposed as druggable NP target; FTY720/LPA-antagonists highlighted [Langeslag M & Kress M 2020, Expert Opin Ther Targets, PMID 32589067]. Fingolimod therapeutic in Cx32-deficient CMT1X model [Biochem Biophys Res Commun 2025, pii S0960896625000720]. Cancer-induced bone pain mitigated via S1P/S1PR1 axis [Grenald SA et al. 2017, Pain 158:1733-42]. Class safety: dose-dependent lymphocyte trapping (functional antagonism after phosphorylation by SPHK2; bradycardia first-dose) [pharmacology reviews incl. PMC3312481; Wikipedia S1PR modulator page].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0052 (lymphopenia to ~20-30% baseline via lymph-node trapping) → **SUPPORTS**. Analgesia literature **EXTENDS** the implied S1P dossier thesis (S1PR1 as analgesic target) across EAE, paclitaxel, bone-cancer and CMT1X models.

### F8.10: Resolution-of-pain gains a systems layer — gut microbiome and neuromodulation induce SPMs
- **Evidence:** Nature Reviews Neurology highlight reviews immune mechanisms contributing to *resolution* of chronic neuropathic pain, including contributions of the gut microbiome and SPMs [Fiore et al. 2025, Nat Rev Neurol, via nature.com chronic-pain subject feed]. Vagus/auricular stimulation increases resolvin levels and attenuates chemotherapy-induced NP [Tao X, Lee MS, Donnelly CR, Ji RR 2020, Neurotherapeutics 17:886-99, doi:10.1007/s13311-020-00892-9].
- **Relation to KB:** **NEW_MECHANISM** (microbiome→SPM supply axis; device-induced endogenous SPM induction) — neither represented among the 8 filtered claims.

## Contradictions & Contested Areas
1. **CB2 bench-bedside gap (strongest tension):** robust, tolerance-free preclinical efficacy (incl. QC-0008's exact experiment) vs LY2828360 having "lacked both toxicity and efficacy" in an osteoarthritis RCT [Pereira 2013, as characterized in PMID 29192123], and documented inconsistent human CB2 trials [PMC12694584]. KB's CB2 dossier should carry an explicit translational-risk flag; condition-match (neuropathic vs OA) is the live hypothesis being tested by CNTX-6016.
2. **SPM drugability:** SPMs are unstable and expensive to produce, limiting direct clinical application [Tao/Ji 2020, Neurotherapeutics]; omega-3 precursor RCT null in RA pain [Sci Rep 2017] — resolution biology is solid, resolvin-as-drug is not yet demonstrated.
3. **Mechanism attribution for DMF:** Nrf2-independent actions (HCAR2, NF-κB, AMPK-Sirt1) mean "DMF works ⇒ Nrf2 works" is unsafe logic [Antioxidants 2024; JCI 2014].
4. **TZD class safety:** rosiglitazone cardiovascular signal, fluid retention, weight gain constrain chronic use for pain [Trends Pharmacol Sci 2024] — pioglitazone favored but not exempt.
5. **Isoform specificity within HO system:** HO-2 is pronociceptive while HO-1 is antinociceptive [PLoS One 2012; PAIN Reports 2018] — any graph statement about "HO activity" must be isoform-resolved.
6. **No direct contradictions found against any of the 8 KB claims' factual content.**
