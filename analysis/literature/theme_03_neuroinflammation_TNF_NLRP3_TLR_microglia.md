## Findings vs Existing Claims

### F3.1: Soluble-TNF/TNFR1 selective inhibition relieves peripheral nerve injury pain — XPro1595 validated in vivo
- **Evidence:** XPro1595 (selective solTNF neutralizer sparing tmTNF/TNFR2) reduced mechanical hypersensitivity in a mouse sciatic PNI model, phenocopied by TNFR1 knockout [MDPI *Cells* 14(22):1749, 2025, doi:10.3390/cells14221749]. Consistent with XPro1595 reversing hindpaw allodynia in Gulf War Illness rats [bioRxiv 2022.11.28.518204] and attenuating bone-cancer pain via spinal p38 MAPK in glia [Zhou et al. 2019, cited in ALZforerum drug summary https://www.alzforum.org/therapeutics/xpro1595].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0117 (XPro1595 selectively blocks solTNF/TNFR1), EC-0083 (NF-κB activates pronociceptive TNFR1) → **SUPPORTS**. EXTENDS: efficacy is sex-dependent — XPro1595 accelerated recovery in male but not female CCI mice (estrogen blunts response; ovariectomy restores it) [Del Rivero et al. 2019, cited in alzdiscovery.org update 2024]; late (2-week) intrathecal dosing after high-thoracic SCI failed for sympathetic hyperreflexia → therapeutic-window constraint [O'Reilly et al., *J Neurotrauma* 38(15):2186-2191, 2021].

### F3.2: Clinical translation of solTNF-selective TNF inhibition is underway (MINDFuL phase 2)
- **Evidence:** Phase 2 MINDFuL trial of XPro1595 in early Alzheimer's with inflammation reported biomarker modulation (↓GFAP neuroinflammation, altered pTau217) with an ARIA-free safety profile at ~1 mg/kg weekly SC [Jaeger et al., *npj Dementia* 2:37, 2026, doi:10.1038/s44400-026-00091-x]. No pain indication yet; earlier COVID-19 Ph2/3 was terminated for futility.
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0117 → **EXTENDS** (first-in-class human safety/PK precedent for the exact molecule in KB's graph; strengthens druggability rationale).

### F3.3: TNFR2 is the pro-resolving arm — agonism as non-addictive analgesia; blanket TNF blockade can impair resolution
- **Evidence:** TNFR2 activation promotes Treg-mediated recovery from neuropathic pain across sexes [*PNAS* 116(37), 2019, doi:10.1073/pnas.1902091116]; hippocampal Nex⁺/Neurod6⁺ pyramidal-neuron TNFR2 is required for spontaneous recovery after CCI, and a TNFR2-selective agonist resolves established pain via β-endorphin (POMC) + endocannabinoid/CB1 signaling [bioRxiv 2025.11.24.690195, doi:10.1101/2025.11.24.690195]; conversely, early local TNFα inhibition after plantar incision delayed pain resolution ("robust acute inflammation initiates resolution") [*J Pain Res*, Dovepress, 2025-11-12]. Selective TNFR1 small-molecule scaffolds exist (zafirlukast analogues) [*ACS Bio Med Chem Au*, 2023, doi:10.1021/acsbiomedchemau.2c00048].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0083/EC-0117 → **SUPPORTS** receptor dichotomy (TNFR1 pronociceptive / TNFR2 protective). EC-0127 ("anti-TNF can paradoxically trigger neuropathies") → **SUPPORTS** and **EXTENDS**: two distinct hazards of indiscriminate anti-TNF — demyelination [Kucharz & Kotulska-Kucharz, *Reumatologia* 59:65-67, 2021, cited in MINDFuL paper] and impaired acute-to-chronic pain resolution. **NEW_MECHANISM:** neuronal TNFR2→POMC/β-endorphin + CB1 pro-resolution axis.

### F3.4: NLRP3 inflammasome is a robust, multi-model pronociceptive node; MCC950 works via microglial pyroptosis and autophagy
- **Evidence:** ICV MCC950 and Nlrp3 KO improved SNI-induced chronic pain + depression comorbidity by restoring autophagy and reducing ACC NLRP3/Caspase-1/IL-1β [*Sci Rep* 16:4932, 2026, doi:10.1038/s41598-026-35400-0]; DRG NLRP3 upregulated via TET2-driven DNA demethylation in diabetic neuropathic pain, MCC950 (and CY-09) relieved allodynia [*J Neuroinflammation* 19:302, 2022, doi:10.1186/s12974-022-02669-7]; electroacupuncture suppressed microglial pyroptosis (NLRP3/caspase-1/GSDMD/IL-18) in CCI [*J Neuroimmune Pharmacol*-indexed PMC11895692, 2025]; morphine prolongs CCI allodynia months via spinal NLRP3/IL-1β [*PNAS* 113(26), 2016, doi:10.1073/pnas.1602070113].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0002 (ANA-12 inhibits NLRP3 inflammasome) → **PARTIAL SUPPORT** of theme directionality only: literature strongly supports NLRP3 as pronociceptive target, but the specific ANA-12→NLRP3 inhibitory edge was not independently confirmed in this sweep (source_note ana-12.md remains UNVERIFIED externally). EC-0079 (mitochondrial dysfunction → TNFα) → **SUPPORTS** indirectly (mtROS/mtDNA are canonical NLRP3 activators). **NEW_MECHANISM:** gasdermin-D pyroptosis downstream of NLRP3 in spinal microglia.

### F3.5: Contested necessity of NLRP3 + MCC950 liabilities
- **Evidence:** In SNI, no elevation of NLRP3 components and unchanged behavior in Nlrp3−/− mice [Curto-Reyes et al. 2015, tabulated in *Front Physiol* 11:1016 review, doi:10.3389/fphys.2020.01016]; MCC950 reported to enhance CD68⁺ cell accumulation with adverse renal effects and raise liver enzymes in clinical studies [citations within Zhang et al. *Sci Rep* 2026; cf. *Eur J Pharmacol* 928:175051, 2022].
- **Relation to KB:** EC-0002 → flags **CONTRADICTION** risk: NLRP3 contribution is model-dependent (positive: CCI, DNP, cancer, EAE, migraine; null: SNI). KB should annotate model scope.

### F3.6: TLR antagonism — TLR4 central, MD2 co-receptor targetable, (+)-naloxone repurposing; TLR2/TLR9 adjuncts
- **Evidence:** TLR4 antagonists (TAK242, Eritoran/E5564) have human safety data but zero neuropathic-pain trials to date; spinal TLR4 drives NF-κB→TNF/IL-1β and acute-to-chronic conversion [review PMC7890571, *J Neuroimmune Pharmacol* 2021]; (+)-naloxone/(−)-naltrexone act as TLR4 antagonists reversing established CCI pain [Hutchinson et al., *Eur J Neurosci* 28:20-29, 2008, doi:10.1111/j.1460-9568.2008.06321.x]; ACT001 orally crosses BBB, binds MD2, blocks TLR4/NF-κB-MAPK, attenuates CCI allodynia [*Front Immunol* 13:873054, 2022, doi:10.3389/fimmu.2022.873054]; dual TLR2/TLR4 blockade potentiates buprenorphine [*Neural Plast* 2016:5238730]; TLR9 antagonist CpG ODN 2088 reduces SCI pain [*Exp Neurol* 2013, S096999611300003X].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0056 (TLR2 time-course quantitative statement) → **SUPPORTS** (upregulation over CCI/SNI course confirmed). No direct KB edge on TLR4 → **NOVEL_TARGET** candidates: ACT001/MD2 axis, (+)-naloxone. **EXTENDS:** caveat that peripheral TLR4 stimulation can transiently release opioid peptides — antagonism may transiently worsen some pain states.

### F3.7: CSF1-CSF1R axis — microglial depletion reverses injury-triggered pain; sex-dimorphic efficacy
- **Evidence:** CSF1R inhibitor (PLX5622-type) treatment reversed injury-triggered neuropathic pain [Lee et al., *Mol Pain* 14, 2018, doi:10.1177/1744806918764979]; PLX5622 is brain-penetrant (~20%) enabling >99% sustained depletion [*Nat Commun* 2019, doi:10.1038/s41467-019-11674-z]; depletion during the chronic phase modifies established pain states [Henry et al., *J Neurosci* 40(14):2960, 2020]; male mice deplete more than females under identical PLX3397 regimens (females compensate via mitochondrial-survival programs) [*Cell Reports* S2211-1247(24)01527-4, 2025].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0012/QC-0013 (CSF1-CSF1R dossier: PLX5622 IC₅₀, pSNL timing) → **SUPPORTS**. **EXTENDS:** systemic myeloid effects outside CNS and hepatotoxicity warning for approved pexidartinib constrain translation.

### F3.8: P2X7 antagonism reaches the clinical threshold — AK1780/LY3857210
- **Evidence:** AK1780 (LY3857210), highly selective CNS-penetrant P2X7 antagonist (primary microglia IC₅₀ ≈ 19.7 nM), dose-dependent analgesia in rat CCI correlated with brain receptor occupancy, no CNS side effects to 150× effective dose, positioned for proof-of-concept NP trials [*J Pain*, 2025, doi:10.1016/j.jpain.2025.105380 / S1526590025006078]; field review prioritizes CNS-penetrant oral P2X7 antagonists [*Curr Pain Rep*, 2026, doi:10.1007/s11916-026-01511-6]; foundational KO data reconfirmed (Chessell et al., *Pain* 114:386-396, 2005).
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0047/QC-0084 (P2X7 low ATP affinity, >100 µM threshold) → **SUPPORTS**. **NOVEL_TARGET:** AK1780 as first P2X7 candidate explicitly advancing toward neuropathic-pain PoC trials.

### F3.9: CX3CL1/CX3CR1 — classic pronociceptive view now formally challenged by transgenic data
- **Evidence:** Canonical pathway: neuronal CX3CL1 cleaved by cathepsin S → soluble fragment activates microglial CX3CR1 → p38 MAPK → allodynia; CX3CR1-KO mice fail to develop allodynia [Silva & Malcangio, *Front Pain Res* 2:684684, 2021, doi:10.3389/fpain.2021.684684; Verge et al., *Eur J Neurosci* 20:1150-1160, 2004]. NEW: CX3CL1-transgenic mice show enhanced signaling MITIGATES neuroinflammation and neuropathic pain-like behavior via dual routes — N-terminal/CX3CR1-dependent and C-terminal/TGF-β-Smad2/3-dependent [PubMed 42461101, 2026].
- **Relation to KB:** No direct KB claim on this axis (KB holds CXCL12-CXCR4, QC-0017 — distinct chemokine pair). → **CONTRADICTION** (fragment- and context-dependent bidirectionality) + **NEW_MECHANISM** (CX3CL1 C-term → TGFβ/Smad2/3 anti-inflammatory route).

### F3.10: Microglial senescence/SASP as driver of pain chronicity and comorbidities
- **Evidence:** Microglial senescence correlates with neuropathic-pain-associated comorbidities in SNI; rosmarinic acid reduced senescence markers [Borgonetti & Galeotti, *Pain* 164(5):1106-1117, 2023, doi:10.1097/j.pain.0000000000002807]; integrative review proposes a self-reinforcing "senescent-pain network": SASP (IL-6, IL-1β, TNFα), mitochondrial dysfunction, DNA damage, epigenetic drift; senotherapy (senolytics/SASP modulators) proposed [*Neurosci Biobehav Rev*, 2026, S0149763426002460]; senolytic ABT-737 cleared p16-high senescent microglia, restoring CA1 synapses after LPS [*Aging Cell*, 2025, doi:10.1111/acel.70167]; microglial remodeling of dentate gyrus links chronic pain to depression biphasically [*Science* 391(6791):eaee6177, 2026, doi:10.1126/science.aee6177].
- **Relation to KB:** QC-0082–QC-0087 (microglial_biology_pain_update: TREM2 downregulation, AL002a, glycolysis/2-DG, urolithin A mitophagy) → **EXTENDS** into the aging/senescence dimension. **NEW_MECHANISM** + **NOVEL_TARGET** (senolytics, SASP modulation) absent from current graph.

### F3.11: Calprotectin (S100A8/A9) — DAMP bridge between damage, TLR4/RAGE and cytokines
- **Evidence:** S100A8/A9 binds both TLR4 and RAGE on BV-2 microglia → ERK/JNK-NF-κB → TNFα/IL-6 secretion [Ma et al., *Int J Mol Med* 40:31-38, 2017, doi:10.3892/ijmm.2017.2987]; calprotectin is an endogenous TLR4 agonist promoting endotoxin shock [Vogl et al., cited PubMed 19451397]; in diabetic neuropathic pain, AGE-RAGE/TLR4/NLRP3 drives microglial IL-1β and BDNF release [*Life Sci*-indexed review, 2026, S0143417926000284].
- **Relation to KB:** No existing claim/note nodes → **NEW_MECHANISM**. Links EC-0083 (NF-kB master driver) upstream to DAMP sensing.

### F3.12: IL-1β downstream blockade — clinical attempts exist but remain sparse
- **Evidence:** Intrathecal IL-1 receptor antagonist reverses established CCI pain in BOTH sexes; NLRP3 expression cell-specific (microglia/astrocytes, not neurons) [Green-Fulgham et al., *Brain Behav Immun*, 2024, PMC10841465]; chronic IL-1 blockade prevents/reverses NP behavior in mice [Wolf et al., *Eur J Pain*, doi:10.1016/j.ejpain.2010.07.012]; anakinra trial registered for long-term CRPS [ISRCTN12908996, 2021].
- **Relation to KB:** Supports the inflammasome→IL-1β output edges implied by EC-0002 theme; **EXTENDS** with translational gap note (no completed NP indication trial surfaced).

## Contradictions & Contested Areas
1. **NLRP3 model-dependence:** null in SNI (Curto-Reyes 2015) vs positive in CCI/DNP/cancer/EAE — any KB claim should carry model annotations.
2. **MCC950 safety:** renal CD68⁺ accumulation, liver-enzyme elevations; off-target debate ongoing.
3. **P2X4-BDNF microglial pathway:** presence of microglial BDNF/P2X4 questioned; IRF5/P2X4 male-only — affects any BDNF↔microglia edges (Coull/Tsuda lineage).
4. **CX3CL1 duality:** soluble-N-term pronociceptive vs enhanced full-length/transgenic protective (TGFβ route) — direct conflict with canonical reading.
5. **Blanket anti-TNF double hazard:** demyelinating neuropathy induction + impaired acute pain resolution — validates KB's EC-0127 cautionary rationale but adds the resolution mechanism.
6. **Pervasive sexual dimorphism:** XPro1595 (males only), TNFR1-blockade (males only), CSF1R-depletion efficacy (male>female), TLR4 contributions variable in females.
