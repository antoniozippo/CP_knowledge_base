## Top-Tier Hypotheses (testable, high-impact)

### H1: Resolvin D1 is an endogenous antagonist of the microglial BDNF–TrkB–KCC2 disinhibition cascade
- **Statement:** SPM signaling (RvD1 via ALX/FPR2) relieves neuropathic allodynia in part by suppressing microglial BDNF release and TrkB-driven KCC2 downregulation, i.e., resolution pharmacology and disinhibition reversal are the same mechanism viewed from two directions.
- **Origin:** Theme 8 F8.2 ↔ Theme 1 F1.10 cross-bridge; validation-report "free wins" bridge (RvD1 ⊣ microglial BDNF/TrkB); extends EC-0014, EC-0017; adjacent to LC-0043.
- **Rationale:** The BDNF–TrkB–KCC2 cascade is the KB's most validated pathway — now human-tissue anchored [SWEEP: Davis 2025 human SDH, PMID 40667986; Dedek, *Brain* 2019] and confirmed for dynamic allodynia via microglial BDNF [SWEEP: Mol Pain 2023, doi:10.1177/17448069231185439]. Independently, RvD1 was reported to inhibit microglial activation *through the BDNF/TrkB pathway* [SWEEP: Eur J Med Res 2025;30:189] and acts via ALX/FPR2 on NLRP3/ERK [SWEEP: Wang, *Neuroscience* 2022]. If RvD1's analgesic target is the microglial TrkB axis, then ANA-12 (validated tool, IC₅₀ ≈45 nM [KB-CONFIRMED]) is the pharmacological mimic and SPM deficiency is the endogenous cause. Spinal cord stimulation raising central RvD1 [SWEEP: Zhang 2021, in Ji 2023 review] supplies a device-based induction route; HRDD gives a human pharmacodynamic readout of spinal KCC2 function [SWEEP: Marshall, *Brain Commun* 2023].
- **Supporting evidence:** Eur J Med Res 2025;30:189 (RvD1–BDNF/TrkB); Wang et al. *Neuroscience* 494:12–24, 2022 (ALX/FPR2–NLRP3/ERK); Mol Pain 19, 2023 (microglial BDNF→dynamic allodynia); Coull 2005 (*Nature* 438:1017) landmark; Davis 2025 (human SDH KCC2 loss); Marshall et al. *Brain Commun* 2023 (HRDD); Ji RR, *Annu Rev Pharmacol Toxicol* 63:273, 2023 (resolution pharmacology frame); Zhang 2021 (SCS↑RvD1); Tao/Ji *Neurotherapeutics* 2020 (VNS induces resolvins).
- **How to test:** In SNI mice: (1) time-course co-measurement of spinal RvD1/17-HDHA levels and membrane-KCC2 (biotinylation) — predict inverse correlation preceding allodynia onset; (2) occlusion experiment — intrathecal RvD1 should add no analgesia on top of ANA-12 if both act on the same axis; (3) ALX/FPR2 knockout should abolish RvD1's protection of KCC2 but not ANA-12's; (4) human: HRDD before/after omega-3-SPM supplementation in painful-DNP patients.
- **Risk/alternative:** RvD1's dominant analgesic target may be TRP channels or cytokines, with BDNF/TrkB inhibition epiphenomenal; microglial P2X4-BDNF presence itself is contested (IRF5/P2X4 male-specific [SWEEP: Malcangio 2018 viewpoint]), which would restrict the axis to males.

### H2: Mechanical allodynia requires a double hit on lamina I projection neurons — microglial PNN stripping plus KCC2-dependent loss of inhibitory control
- **Statement:** Allodynia emerges only when microglial GAG-stripping of perineuronal nets around NK1R⁺ spinoparabrachial projection neurons coincides with weakened inhibitory input (KCC2-impaired interneuron output) onto those same cells; either perturbation alone is compensated.
- **Origin:** EC-0070 contradiction resolution (Theme 2 F2.3; Theme 1 F1.13); LC-0053 (PV-KCC2 ↔ NK1R bridge); LC-0024 (MMP-9 hub ↔ disinhibition cascade).
- **Rationale:** Tansley 2022 reassigned pronociceptive PNN degradation from PV interneurons to lamina I spinoparabrachial projection neurons — microglia strip GAGs while aggrecan cores survive, and local chABC reproduces spontaneous pain + thermal hypersensitivity [SWEEP: *Science* 377:80]. In parallel, disinhibition via KCC2 downregulation is human-anchored [SWEEP: Davis 2025] and PV firing-pattern restoration attenuates allodynia [SWEEP: Qiu, *PNAS* 2024]. The two mechanisms converge anatomically on projection neurons: PNN removal increases their intrinsic excitability, while KCC2 failure removes GABAergic restraint from PV/NPY interneuron populations onto them. A dual-hit model explains why neither lesion alone fully converts animals (compensation window) and predicts synergy measurable at the projection-neuron firing level. The NK1R⁺ identity of the relevant projection class is classical but should be re-verified against the Tansley population.
- **Supporting evidence:** Tansley et al. *Science* 2022;377:80–86 (GAG stripping, both sexes); Müller & Claff, *STTGT* 2022 (commentary); Qiu et al. *PNAS* 2024 (PV firing-pattern gate); Rankin et al. *Cell Rep* 2024 (network decorrelation); Mol Pain 19, 2023 (microglial BDNF→allodynia); Mapplebeck *Cell Rep* 2019 (KCC2 both sexes); Sánchez-Ventura *IJMS* 2024 (Crtl1/PNN integrity necessity); Tuina/chABC Ca²⁺-overload demonstration, *Front Mol Neurosci* 2026 (doi:10.3389/fnmol.2026.1860375).
- **How to test:** Intersectional genetics in mice: WFA/NK1R co-immunostaining to confirm the stripped population is NK1R⁺; then conditionally restore KCC2 (AAV-KCC2-G42x enhancer-resistant variant) in PV+ or NPY+ interneurons while inducing lamina-I PNN loss (chABC microinject) — dual-hit animals should show suprasummary allodynia vs either perturbation; read out spinoparabrachial neuron Ca²⁺ transients in behaving mice.
- **Risk/alternative:** Single-hit sufficiency — if chABC alone or PV-ablation alone reliably induces full allodynia without the other perturbation, the compensation model dies; alternatively the decisive inhibitory deficit may be dynorphinergic gating (Dyn+ → SOM+ circuit, Duan 2014 [SWEEP]) rather than PV/KCC2.

### H3: Histone lactylation is the chromatin permission step that opens the injury-phase MMP-9 switch (ELF1/DRGX-controlled)
- **Statement:** Early-phase allodynia requires lactylation marks (H3K18la/H4K8la) deposited at the *Mmp9* locus that license ELF1-driven transcription after DRGX derepression; blocking lactylation enzymes within the early window prevents MMP-9 induction and chronification.
- **Origin:** LC-0049 (proBDNF/p75 ↔ H3K18la bridge, transformed); Theme 2 F2.2 (ELF1→MMP-9) + F2.10 (phase framework); Theme 6 F6.7 (DRGX polarity inversion); Theme 9 F9.1/F9.3 (lactylation machinery); extends EC-0070–EC-0074, QC-0071; corrects EC-0031–EC-0034.
- **Rationale:** Three validated findings snap together: (1) MMP-9's pronociceptive action is phase-structured — rapid/transient DRG upregulation gates the early phase (Kawasaki 2008 intact through 2025 reviews) [SWEEP]; (2) ELF1 is the injury-induced transcriptional activator of *Mmp9*, shRNA-blockable in both sexes [SWEEP: PMC9633582], and DRGX normally restrains MMP-9 — its loss after SNL derepresses the locus [SWEEP: Mol Pain 2020, corrected polarity]; (3) the lactylation machinery (LDHA→lactate→p300→H3K18la) is causal in injured DRG and pharmacologically reversible [SWEEP: Wang, *J Neurosci* 2025, IGF2BP2; Deng, *Sci Signal* 2025, AREG-EGFR-PKM2]. The untested junction is chromatin-level: whether *Mmp9* is a direct lactylation target whose marks rise in the early window and fall by the late (MMP-2-dominated) phase. If true, glycolysis/lactylation inhibitors (2-DG, oxamate, FX11-class) become windowed preventives for the acute-to-chronic transition — matching the prevention-of-chronification protocol logic already in the KB (EC-0120).
- **Supporting evidence:** Kawasaki et al. *Nat Med* 2008;14:331 (phase structure); ELF1/*Mmp9* study, PMC9633582; Mol Pain 2020;16:33 (DRGX restrains MMP-9; knockdown induces pain); Wang Q-H et al. *J Neurosci* 2025, DOI 10.1523/jneurosci.0365-25.2025 (H3K18la→IGF2BP2 causal, 2-DG-sensitive); Deng et al. *Sci Signal* 2025;18(891):eadr9397 (AREG→glycolysis→lactylation); Front Mol Neurosci 2025 MMP-9 review, doi:10.3389/fnmol.2025.1647316; QC-0071 model-specificity annotation (validation report).
- **How to test:** ChIP-qPCR/CUT&RUN for H3K18la, H4K8la, p300 and ELF1 at the *Mmp9* promoter in L4/5 DRG at 6 h/24 h/72 h/7 d post-SNI (predict: early peak, decay before day 7); block with intrathecal 2-DG or DRG-specific LDHA knockdown and measure *Mmp9* mRNA induction + early allodynia; reciprocal: sodium lactate into uninjured DRG ± ELF1 overexpression should suffice to induce MMP-9.
- **Risk/alternative:** Lactylation never touches the *Mmp9* locus (acts only via *Igf2bp2*/*Dock4*-type targets) and MMP-9 induction is purely TF-level; or the marks are model-specific (H3K18la robust in CCI but unchanged in SNL per QC-0071 annotation) — the hypothesis then narrows to a CCI-type subset.

### H4: RNA-regulatory convergence sets Nav1.7 surface density — explaining why pure channel blockade under-delivers
- **Statement:** Human nociceptor excitability is governed by coordinated RNA-level brakes on Nav1.7 — Rbfox1-dependent *SCN9A* mRNA stability/splicing, H4K8la-lactylation-repressed DOCK4 trafficking scaffolding, and IGF2BP2-dependent m6A stabilization — and their joint failure, not channel gain alone, caps the efficacy of Nav1.8/Nav1.7 blockers.
- **Origin:** LC-0050 (IGF2BP2 ↔ Rbfox1 bridge); LC-0028 (NrCAM exon-10 ↔ SCN9A-stability dual arm); Theme 9 F9.1+F9.3; Theme 6 F6.1/F6.2/F6.6; extends EC-0103–EC-0105, QC-0066/QC-0088.
- **Rationale:** Suzetrigine met Phase 3 primary endpoints yet failed superiority over hydrocodone/APAP, with human DRG physiology showing Nav1.7 retains AP initiation under >99% Nav1.8 occupancy [SWEEP: Bertoch, *Anesthesiology* 2025; Stewart, *PNAS* 2025; Vasylyev/Waxman, *JGP* 2024]. Meanwhile three independent RNA-regulatory layers controlling sodium-channel biology surfaced in one round: Rbfox1 loss destabilizes *Scn9a* mRNA and switches *Nrcam* exon 10 (ASO-reversible in 72 h) [SWEEP: He, *Neurotherapeutics* 2023; Liang, *J Pain* 2020]; H4K8la represses DOCK4, disrupting Dynein/DOCK4/Nav1.7 trafficking and elevating heat pain in mice and male macaques [SWEEP: *Nat Commun* 2025, doi:10.1038/s41467-025-62343-3]; and the lactylation→IGF2BP2→m6A-reader arm stabilizes pronociceptive transcripts [SWEEP: Wang, *J Neurosci* 2025; METTL14/IGF2BP2/GluN2A, *JCI* 174847]. The hypothesis makes the field's multi-target intuition (PNAS commentary, PMC12232689) mechanistically precise: combination therapy should couple a channel blocker with an RNA-regulatory corrector (e.g., ASO restoring Rbfox1 targets, or lactylation blockade restoring DOCK4).
- **Supporting evidence:** Bertoch et al. *Anesthesiology* 2025 (PMID 40117446); Stewart et al. *PNAS* 2025;122:e2503570122; He et al. *Neurotherapeutics* 2023 (PMID 38241164); Liang et al. *J Pain* 2020 (PMID 31917219); *Nat Commun* 2025 DOCK4 paper (PMC12322138); Wang et al. *J Neurosci* 2025 (IGF2BP2); METTL14–IGF2BP2–GluN2A, *J Clin Invest* 174847; PNAS commentary 2025 (PMC12232689).
- **How to test:** m6A-RIP-seq + RNA-half-life measurement of *SCN9A* in control vs injured human iPSC-noceceptors ± IGF2BP2 knockdown; in Rbfox1-conditional DRG mice, test whether suzetrigine's AP-amplitude reduction is rescued/blunted when Nav1.7 conductance is elevated by Rbfox1 loss; behavioral: combined subthreshold suzetrigine + intrathecal DOCK4-restoring oxamate should exceed either alone in SNL heat hypersensitivity.
- **Risk/alternative:** The layers are independent and additive rather than convergent on Nav1.7 homeostasis; IGF2BP2 may not bind *SCN9A* at all (its validated cargo is *Cct2*), reducing H4 to a metaphor.

### H5: TIAM1 — not Kalirin-7 — is the Rac1-GEF mediating ACC structural plasticity in chronic pain; spinal Kal7 is pronociceptive, so Kal7 restoration would backfire
- **Statement:** ACC dendritic-spine remodeling and pain-affect sensitization after nerve injury are mediated by TIAM1–Rac1 (plus AC1/GluN2B/HCN pre-LTP), while Kalirin-7's only reproducible pain role is pronociceptive recruitment in the dorsal horn — therefore the KB's Kal7-restoration therapy must be replaced by TIAM1-focused or circuit-level strategies.
- **Origin:** Validation-report adjudications #2 (no ACC-Kal7 study exists; TIAM1 identified, Ru et al., *JCI* 2022;132:e158545) + Theme 10 F10.1/F10.2; corrects EC-0058/EC-0059 (REFUTED) and demotes EC-0054–EC-0068, EC-0147, EC-0157, EC-0160–EC-0163 to hypothesis nodes.
- **Rationale:** A systematic PubMed enumeration (`kalirin OR KALRN "pain"`) returns ~10 papers, all spinal, all pronociceptive (Kal7 gain-of-function drives sensitization; knockdown relieves) [SWEEP: *Nat Commun* 2015, ncomms7820 + SGK1/SIRT1/T2D/fracture follow-ups]. The phenomenon the KB wanted to explain — ACC spine loss, anxiety-selective plasticity — is real but carries a different molecular signature: two-LTP architecture (kainate/AC1/HCN pre-LTP ↔ anxiety; NMDAR post-LTP ↔ affect) [SWEEP: Koga, *Neuron* 2015; Bliss et al., *Nat Rev Neurosci* 2016], microglial P2X4R–BDNF–TrkB feeding ACC hyperactivity [SWEEP: Liang, *Br J Anaesth* 2025], and PSD-95–NR2B interdependence [SWEEP: Li A, *Sci Rep* 2022]. TIAM1 emerged as the evidenced ACC Rac1-GEF from the targeted adjudication search [ADJUDICATION: Ru et al., *JCI* 2022]. Sign risk is asymmetric: broad CNS Kal7 enhancement (the old KB proposal) would be predicted to worsen spinal sensitization even if ACC-affect improved — a falsifiable harm prediction.
- **Supporting evidence:** *Nat Commun* 2015;6:7820 (spinal Kal7 pronociceptive); Ru et al. *J Clin Invest* 2022;132:e158545 (TIAM1 in ACC, via adjudication search); Koga et al. *Neuron* 2015;85:377 (two-LTP); Liang et al. *Br J Anaesth* 2025;135:710 (P2X4R-BDNF-ACC); Li A et al. *Sci Rep* 2022 (PSD-95/NR2B ACC); Mol Brain 2021 (NB001 blocks BDNF-ACC synaptic enhancement); NSC23766 off-target caveats (JPET 2013 PMID 23887096; *J Neurosci* 2014;34:14006).
- **How to test:** Conditional TIAM1 deletion in ACC excitatory neurons (vs dorsal-horn controls) after SNI — predict reduced ACC spine gain/pre-LTP and anxiety phenotypes with spared acute nociception; mirror experiment for Kal7 (predict: ACC manipulation ineffective, spinal Kal7 knockdown analgesic); quantify Rac1-GTP pulldown from ACC synaptosomes.
- **Risk/alternative:** A region-divergent Kal7 role exists but is unpublished (query-relative negative); TIAM1 in ACC may serve development rather than adult plasticity, leaving the ACC Rac1-GEF slot vacant.

### H6: TNFR2-selective agonism resolves established neuropathic pain via neuronal β-endorphin and CB1 signaling — the anti-anti-TNF strategy
- **Statement:** Selective TNFR2 activation engages Nex⁺-neuron POMC/β-endorphin release and endocannabinoid/CB1 signaling to resolve established chronic pain, whereas indiscriminate TNF blockade delays resolution and risks demyelination.
- **Origin:** Theme 3 F3.3 (NEW_MECHANISM); extends EC-0127 (anti-TNF double hazard); adjacent to LC-0009/LC-0046 (TNFR1-XPro1595 nodes, retained as contrast arm).
- **Rationale:** The TNF system is bimodal: TNFR1 is pronociceptive and solTNF-selective blockade works preclinically (XPro1595 validated in PNI models, sex-dependent) [KB-CONFIRMED + SWEEP: *Cells* 2025], while TNFR2 mediates Treg-driven recovery across sexes [SWEEP: *PNAS* 2019] and — in new preprint data — hippocampal Nex⁺/Neurod6⁺ pyramidal-neuron TNFR2 is *required for spontaneous recovery*, with a selective agonist resolving established pain via β-endorphin (POMC) + CB1 [PREPRINT: bioRxiv 2025.11.24.690195]. Meanwhile early TNFα inhibition after incision delayed pain resolution [SWEEP: *J Pain Res* 2025] and anti-TNF drugs carry demyelination hazard [SWEEP: Kucharz & Kotulska-Kucharz, *Reumatologia* 2021]. The hypothesis reframes the entire anti-TNF-in-pain program: the target is not less TNF but more TNFR2-biased signaling.
- **Supporting evidence:** *PNAS* 116(37), 2019, doi:10.1073/pnas.1902091116 (TNFR2/Treg resolution); bioRxiv 2025.11.24.690195 (TNFR2→β-endorphin/CB1; PREPRINT); *Cells* 14(22):1749, 2025 (XPro1595/TNFR1 contrast); *J Pain Res* 2025 (blockade delays resolution); npj Dementia 2026 (MINDFuL Ph2 safety precedent for solTNF-selective biologics); ACS Bio Med Chem Au 2023 (zafirlukast-analog TNFR1 scaffolds — selectivity feasibility).
- **How to test:** TNFR2-selective agonist in CCI/SNI after establishment (day 7+) with: Pomc conditional knockout (loss of analgesia predicted), CB1 antagonist occlusion, and TNFR1-intact safety monitoring; biomarker: plasma β-endorphin + spinal POMC immunoreactivity dose-response; extend to female cohort given pervasive male-skew of TNF-axis data.
- **Risk/alternative:** The preprint fails peer review or replication; agonist engagement of peripheral TNFR2 (immune compartments) causes inflammation/safety liabilities; β-endorphin route reduces to ordinary opioid pharmacology with tolerance.

### H7: NO–cGMP–PKG signaling is a third, BDNF-independent route to KCC2 hypofunction in PV interneurons
- **Statement:** Injury-upregulated iNOS-derived NO activates cGMP/PKG in the dorsal horn, which phosphorylates/endocytoses KCC2 preferentially in PV interneurons, producing disinhibition that survives both TrkB blockade and caspase/calpain inhibition.
- **Origin:** LC-0032 (NO-cGMP-PKG ↔ PV KCC2-dependence, highest-jaccard structural bridge in the NO neighborhood); LC-0045/LC-0056 (TrkB ↔ NO module adjacency); builds on KB-CONFIRMED kinase precedents (PKC Thr⁹⁰⁶/Thr¹⁰⁰⁷; calpain cleavage).
- **Rationale:** The KB encodes two validated KCC2-suppressing arms — BDNF/TrkB/MAPK and PKCε/γ phosphorylation, plus calpain cleavage [KB-CONFIRMED; SWEEP: Zhou, *JBC* 2012 via Lee-Hotta 2019] — but the NO-cGMP-PKG community sits structurally adjacent to KCC2 and BDNF with no connecting edge ([STRUCT-ONLY]: LC-0032 shares BDNF+KCC2 neighbors, jaccard 0.67). Nitric-oxide biology is independently pronociceptive in every swept theme (peroxynitrite, iNOS, CORM counter-action [SWEEP: Hervera 2012 — HO-1/CO analgesia partly via inhibiting NO]). Kinase-mediated KCC2 internalization is an established mechanism class, so PKG is a plausible fourth writer. If confirmed, NO-source cells (iNOS⁺ glia) become upstream druggable handles complementary to TrkB antagonists, and the 1400W/iNOS node gains a specific disinhibition endpoint.
- **Supporting evidence:** LC-0032 structural basis; Zhou et al. *JBC* 2012 (calpain/KCC2 proteolysis precedent); Physiol Rev 2022 chloride-homeostasis review, doi:10.1152/physrev.00025.2021 (phospho-regulation landscape incl. WNK1); Hervera et al. *PLoS One* 2012 (CO/HO-1 analgesia opposing NO); Mol Pain 2023 (disinhibition readouts available).
- **How to test:** Intratheal NO donor ± sGC/PKG inhibitors (ODQ, KT5823) with KCC2 membrane fractionation and phospho-KCC2 westerns dissected by cell type (PV-TRAP); iNOS−/− or myeloid-iNOS-deletion SNI mice for KCC2 rescue; predict: PKG inhibition preserves PV-KCC2 without touching TrkB phosphorylation.
- **Risk/alternative:** NO affects KCC2 only indirectly (blood flow, glutamate spillover, zinc release); or PKG targets NKCC1 (the documented IL-6-axis target [SWEEP: Pieraut 2011]) rather than KCC2, flipping the prediction.

### H8: ORL1 (nociceptin receptor) gates NgR1 surface maturation — opioid-system pharmacology repurposed as myelin-inhibitor blockade
- **Statement:** Trauma-upregulated ORL1 enhances O-linked glycosylation and surface delivery of immature NgR1, so ORL1 antagonists acutely lower functional NgR1 abundance and synergize with NgR1-directed strategies for recovery and pain after SCI.
- **Origin:** Theme 5 F5.7 (NEW_MECHANISM; Sekine 2018); upstream of every NgR1-centered claim (EC-0106, EC-0095, EC-0151); adjacent to LC-0012 (NgR1 duplicate-node artifact, now merged).
- **Rationale:** NgR1-centric therapy just suffered clinical setbacks — NISCI Phase 2b missed its primary endpoint and opicinumab failed three Phase 2s [SWEEP: *Lancet Neurol* 2025 PMID 39706632; Mult Scler 2025 PMID 41454463], while NgR1's chronic-inhibition role is genetically qualified (acute growth-cone collapse only) [SWEEP: Chivatakarn, *J Neurosci* 2007]. Any new handle on NgR1 abundance is therefore valuable: ORL1 co-immunoprecipitates with immature NgR1, drives its maturation, and J113397 additively improves locomotor recovery with NgR1 deletion [SWEEP: Sekine, *Sci Signal* 2018;11:eaao4180]. Because ORL1 is an opioid-family GPCR with CNS-penetrant antagonist chemistry, it offers a small-molecule route into a space dominated by intrathecal antibodies. The hypothesis also predicts ORL1-antagonist effects on NgR1-dependent TRPV1 stabilization (Nogo-A/NgR1→TRPV1 lineage [SWEEP: Li 2014/Hu 2019/Chen 2021]).
- **Supporting evidence:** Sekine et al. *Sci Signal* 2018;11(559):eaao4180; Chivatakarn et al. *J Neurosci* 2007;27:7117 (acute-vs-chronic qualification); NISCI, *Lancet Neurol* 2025 (clinical gap motivating new handles); Chen et al. *IJMS* 2021;22:10360 (CRMP2/microtubule TRPV1 branch downstream of Nogo-A).
- **How to test:** J113397 (or newer ORL1 antagonists) in SNI/SCI with NgR1 surface biotinylation + glycosylation-shift westerns at 24–72 h; behavioral synergy with anti-Nogo-A or soluble NgR1 decoy; predict: ORL1 blockade lowers mature NgR1 without touching PirB/NgR2 (explaining partial, acute-window effects).
- **Risk/alternative:** ORL1 biology is bidirectional (nociceptin can be antinociceptive in supraspinal circuits), so systemic antagonism may trade one pain phenotype for another; the 2018 finding awaits replication.
